# Understanding Your Role

*Your role in Kando determines what you can see and do in the system. This guide explains the different roles, what each can do, and why some features may be unavailable to you.*

## Overview

Every user in Kando has a **role** (also called an "Access Group"). Your role controls:

- ✅ Which menus you see
- ✅ Which features you can use
- ✅ Which data you can view
- ✅ Which actions you can perform

This page explains the four main roles and what each can do.

### Key Concept: Access Groups

Think of your role as a **permission set**. The system checks your role before allowing any action:

```
You try to approve a leave request
    ↓
System checks your role
    ↓
If role = "Manager" or "Owner" → Allowed ✅
If role = "Employee" or "HR Admin" → Not allowed ❌
```

## The Four Main Roles

### 👑 Owner

**Who**: Company leadership, finance staff, system administrators

**Permission Level**: **FULL ACCESS** - Can do everything

#### What Owners Can Do

**System Management**:
- ✅ View all organization settings
- ✅ Add/remove users
- ✅ Configure system policies
- ✅ View all employees across all departments
- ✅ Access billing and subscription
- ✅ Configure access groups and permissions
- ✅ View organization-wide reports

**Employee Actions**:
- ✅ Clock in/out
- ✅ Request leave
- ✅ Submit timesheets
- ✅ View own schedule
- ✅ View own payslip

**Manager Actions**:
- ✅ View all team members
- ✅ Approve/reject all requests
- ✅ Create and manage all schedules
- ✅ View all reports
- ✅ Make adjustments to timesheets and payroll

**Example Owner Tasks**:
- Set up a new organization
- Add 100 new employees
- Configure leave policies
- Adjust someone's timesheet
- Run organization-wide payroll

---

### 👨‍💼 Manager

**Who**: Department leads, team leads, supervisors

**Permission Level**: **TEAM & REPORTS ONLY** - Can manage their team

#### What Managers Can Do

**Team Management**:
- ✅ View their direct reports only
- ✅ View team attendance and timesheets
- ✅ Create and manage team schedules
- ✅ View team reports

**Request Approvals**:
- ✅ Approve/reject leave requests from their team
- ✅ Approve/reject overtime requests
- ✅ Make changes to their team's timesheets (if pending)

**Employee Actions**:
- ✅ Clock in/out (for themselves)
- ✅ Request leave (for themselves)
- ✅ Submit timesheets (for themselves)
- ✅ View own schedule
- ✅ View own payslip

**❌ Cannot Do**:
- ❌ View employees outside their team
- ❌ Access system settings or configuration
- ❌ Add or remove users
- ❌ View other managers' teams
- ❌ Approve requests they're not assigned to
- ❌ View payroll or billing information
- ❌ Change organization-wide policies

**Example Manager Tasks**:
- Approve team member's leave request
- Create next week's schedule
- View team's time tracking
- Adjust timesheet before approval
- View team attendance report

## Why Some Features Are Unavailable

### Scenario 1: "I can't see other employees' data"

This is by design for **data security and privacy**.

- **Employees** can only see their own data
- **Managers** can only see their team's data
- **HR Admins** can see all data (need to manage payroll, policy compliance)
- **Owners** can see all data (system administration)

**Solution**: If you need to see someone else's data, contact someone with higher permission level.

### Scenario 2: "I can't approve this leave request"

Possible reasons:

1. **Your role is not "Manager" or "Owner"**
   - Only managers and owners can approve requests
   - HR admins cannot approve (they configure approval rules)

2. **You're not assigned as an approver**
   - The employee's manager might be someone else
   - Check if the employee is in your team

3. **You're not in the approval workflow**
   - Request might require HR approval after manager approval
   - You might need to wait for other approver

**Solution**: Check the request's approval status. It may show pending with another approver.

### Scenario 3: "I can't access payroll features"

Only **HR Admins** and **Owners** can:
- Create payroll cycles
- Configure pay types
- Finalize payroll
- View payroll reports

**Why**: Payroll requires special permissions for security and compliance.

**Solution**: If you need payroll access, contact your HR department or Owner to assign you "HR Admin" role.

### Scenario 4: "Why can't I see the Settings menu?"

**Settings menu** access depends on your role:

- **Employees** → Can only access own Settings (profile, password, preferences)
- **Managers** → Can see team-related settings only
- **HR Admins** → Can access HR-specific settings (leave types, policies)
- **Owners** → Can access all settings

**Solution**: If you need to change organization settings, contact Owner.

## Permission Model: How It Works

### Three Layers of Permission

Kando uses **three layers** to determine what you can do:

#### Layer 1: Role-Based Access (Your Access Group)

Your role determines what **features** you can access:
```
Role: Manager
  ↓
Can access: Team Management, Approvals, Scheduling
Can access: Time Tracking, Leave
Cannot access: System Settings, Payroll Configuration
```

#### Layer 2: Hierarchy-Based Access (Your Team)

Your position in the organization determines which **data** you can see:
```
You are: Manager
Your team: Employees A, B, C
  ↓
Can see: Timesheets of A, B, C (your team)
Cannot see: Timesheets of D, E, F (other teams)
```

#### Layer 3: Workflow-Based Access (Your Approval Rules)

Your approval assignments determine which **requests** you can act on:
```
Workflow says: Manager must approve, then HR
You are: Manager
  ↓
Can approve: Leave requests assigned to you
Cannot approve: Leave requests assigned to HR only
```

### Permission Check Example

**Scenario**: Manager tries to approve leave request from Employee B

```
Is the user trying to approve a leave request?
  ↓
Check Role: Is user a Manager or Owner?
  → NO → Access Denied ❌
  → YES → Continue
  ↓
Check Hierarchy: Is Employee B in this Manager's team?
  → NO → Access Denied ❌
  → YES → Continue
  ↓
Check Workflow: Is this Manager in the approval chain?
  → NO → Access Denied ❌
  → YES → Allowed ✅
```

## Special Cases: Multi-Role Users

### Can One Person Have Multiple Roles?

**Generally: NO**

Each user has **ONE primary role**:
- User A is "Employee" OR "Manager" OR "HR Admin" OR "Owner" (not multiple)

**Exception**: Workflow-based permissions allow additional actions:
- An **HR Admin** who is also an employee's manager can approve their leave
- An **Owner** can do everything (they have full permissions by definition)

### Example: HR Manager

A person can be "HR Admin" as their primary role, but also:
- Clock in/out (employee actions)
- Request leave (employee actions)
- Approve leave (if assigned as manager for a specific team)

This is because:
1. Their primary role is HR Admin
2. They also have employee permissions automatically
3. They can be assigned as a manager in the approval workflow

## How to Check Your Role

### Method 1: Check Your Profile

1. Log into Kando
2. Click your **Profile Icon** (top-right corner)
3. Select **Profile** or **My Account**
4. Look for **Role** or **Access Group** field
5. You'll see: "Employee", "Manager", "HR Admin", or "Owner"

### Method 2: Check Available Menus

Look at your left sidebar menu:

**If you see**:
- Only: Time, Leave, Schedule, Pay → You're an **Employee**
- Plus: Team Management, Approvals → You're a **Manager**
- Plus: HR Admin settings, Payroll → You're an **HR Admin**
- Plus: Organization Settings, Users → You're an **Owner**

### Method 3: Ask Your Manager or HR

If unsure about your role, contact:
- Your manager
- Your HR department
- Your system administrator

## Common Role Questions & Answers

### "Why can't I do something other people can?"

**Answer**: Your role doesn't have permission for that feature.

**Possible reasons**:
- You need a different role
- You need to be assigned as an approver
- You're not in the required team/hierarchy
- The feature requires higher permissions

**Solution**: Contact your HR department or manager to request role change.

---

### "I'm a manager but can't approve requests"

**Possible reasons**:
1. **Employees not assigned to you**: Their `reporting_to` field doesn't point to you
2. **Not in approval workflow**: The approval rule doesn't include you as approver
3. **Wrong approval workflow**: Multi-step approval may require HR first

**Solution**:
- Check if employees are in your team (in their Employee record, Manager field)
- Ask HR admin if approval rule is configured correctly
- Check the request's approval status to see who else is approving

---

### "Why can't I see employees from other departments?"

**Answer**: By design, managers can only see their own team members.

**Why**: Protects employee privacy and prevents accidental changes to others' data.

**If you need to see others**:
- You need "HR Admin" or "Owner" role
- Contact your HR or Owner to request access

---

### "I need to change my role for a different job"

**Process**:
1. Contact your HR department or Owner
2. Explain your new job responsibilities
3. They will update your role in the system
4. You may need to logout and login again for changes to take effect

**Common transitions**:
- Employee → Manager: Now managing a team
- Manager → HR Admin: Now handling HR tasks
- Any role → Owner: System administration responsibilities

---

### "Who can change my role?"

Only **Owners** can change user roles.

**Process**:
1. Owner goes to **Settings** → **Users** or **Administration** → **Users**
2. Finds your user account
3. Updates your **Access Group** field
4. Saves the change

You'll typically be notified when your role changes.

---

### "What if I have questions about permissions?"

**Support contacts**:
- Your direct manager
- Your HR department
- Your system administrator
- Check [Getting Help](../8-Reference/7.4-Getting-Help.md) page

## Approval Workflows & Your Role

### How Leave Approval Works

The approval process depends on **both** your role and your assignment:

```
Employee requests leave
  ↓
System finds approval rule for this leave type
  ↓
If rule says "Manager approval required":
  - Sends to employee's manager
  - Only that manager can approve
  ↓
If rule says "Manager then HR approval required":
  - First: Sends to manager
  - Manager approves or rejects
  - If manager approves: Sends to HR
  - HR makes final decision
  ↓
If rule says "Auto-approve":
  - Automatically approves
  - Deducts leave credit immediately
  - No action needed from anyone
```

**Your role determines**: Can you be an approver?
**Your assignment determines**: Will this request come to you?

## Field-Level Access

In addition to feature access, some roles have **restricted field visibility**:

**Example: Employee Record**

- **Employee**: Can see: Name, Email, Phone, Department
- **Employee**: Cannot see: Salary, Bank Details, Tax Information
- **Manager**: Can see: Name, Email, Phone, Department, Timesheet
- **Manager**: Cannot see: Salary, Bank Details, Tax Information
- **HR Admin**: Can see: Everything
- **Owner**: Can see: Everything

**Why**: Salary and bank details are sensitive; only HR and Owner need access.

## Access Control Best Practices

✅ **DO**:
- Know what your role can and cannot do
- Ask your manager or HR if you're unsure about permissions
- Report if you can see data you shouldn't have access to
- Update your profile if your job changes
- Request appropriate role change when needed

❌ **DON'T**:
- Ask someone to use their login to do your work
- Share your login credentials with others
- Try to work around permission restrictions
- Assume you have access you're not sure about
- Use someone else's account to approve requests

## What to Do If...

### ...I Need to Approve Requests

**Check your role**:
1. Are you a Manager or Owner? → ✅ You can approve
2. Is the employee in your team? → ✅ You can see their requests
3. Are you in the approval workflow? → ✅ You can act on this request

**If any is NO**: Contact HR to get proper assignment.

### ...I Can't See An Employee's Data

**Check your role**:
1. Are you HR Admin or Owner? → You should see all
2. Are you their Manager? → You should see their team data
3. Are you an Employee? → You should only see your own

**If you need access**: Contact HR or Owner to request.

### ...I'm Not Sure About My Role

**Verify**:
1. Check your profile (click your name → Profile)
2. Look for "Role" or "Access Group" field
3. If unsure, contact your manager or HR

### ...I Need Different Permissions

**Process**:
1. Talk to your manager or HR
2. Explain why you need different permissions
3. They'll submit request to Owner
4. Owner updates your role
5. Changes take effect after logout/login
