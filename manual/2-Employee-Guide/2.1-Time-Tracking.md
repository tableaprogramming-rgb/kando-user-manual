# Time Tracking Guide

Learn how to track your work time, clock in and out, and submit timesheets in Kando.

## What is Time Tracking?

Time tracking records when you work and how long you work. It helps:
- Calculate your pay accurately
- Track attendance
- Identify work patterns
- Ensure accountability
- Plan scheduling

## Clock In / Clock Out

### Clocking In

**Goal**: Start tracking your work time for the day

**Steps**:
1. Open Kando dashboard
2. Click the **Clock In** button (large button at top)
3. You'll see confirmation: "You clocked in at 09:00 AM"
4. Status changes to "Clocked In"

**Keyboard Shortcut**: Press `Ctrl+I` to clock in instantly

### Clocking Out

**Goal**: Stop tracking your work time

**Steps**:
1. When ending work, go to dashboard
2. Click **Clock Out** button
3. You'll see confirmation: "You clocked out at 05:00 PM"
4. Status changes to "Clocked Out"

**Keyboard Shortcut**: Press `Ctrl+O` to clock out instantly

## Common Scenarios

### Forgot to Clock In

**Situation**: You worked all day but forgot to clock in.

**Solution**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Time Entries**
2. Click **Add Manual Entry**
3. Enter:
   - **Date**: Today
   - **Start Time**: When you arrived (e.g., 09:00 AM)
   - **End Time**: When you left (e.g., 05:00 PM)
   - **Notes**: "Manual entry - forgot to clock in"
4. Click **Add Entry**
5. Notify your manager

### Forgot to Clock Out

**Situation**: You clocked in but forgot to clock out.

**Solution**:
1. Go to **Time** → **My Clocking**
2. Find the entry that's still open (shows "Clocked In")
3. Click **Clock Out Now**
4. It will clock you out with current time
5. If wrong time, edit the entry

### Worked Break Time

**Situation**: You took a 1-hour lunch break but clocked out then back in.

**Note**: Your timesheet will show actual working hours, not including breaks.

**To add break time**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Breaks**
2. Click **Add Break**
3. Enter break duration
4. This reduces your total hours automatically

### Overtime

**Situation**: You worked extra hours beyond your normal shift.

**Handling**:
1. Clock in and out normally (captures extra time)
2. When submitting timesheet, overtime is automatically flagged
3. Manager sees overtime and approves
4. Payroll processes overtime pay

## Timesheet Submission

### What is a Timesheet?

A **timesheet** summarizes your work hours for a period (usually 1 week).

It shows:
- Daily hours worked
- Total hours for the period
- Any overtime
- Days off or leave taken
- Status (pending, approved, paid)

### Create Your Timesheet

**When**: Usually Friday or end of period

**Steps**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Timesheets**
2. Click **Create New Timesheet** (or view weekly list)
3. System shows all your clock entries for the week
4. Review each day:
   - Hours are correct?
   - Any manual entries need noting?
   - Matches what you actually worked?
5. Enter **Notes** if needed
6. Click **Submit Timesheet**

### Submit Your Timesheet

**Steps**:
1. Open your timesheet (from dashboard or Time menu)
2. Review all entries
3. Click **Submit for Approval**
4. Add comments if needed
5. Click **Submit** to confirm

**What happens next**:
- Your manager gets a notification
- They review and approve
- You get a confirmation email
- Approved timesheet goes to payroll

### Manage Your Timesheet

**View Status**:
- Go to **Time** → **Timesheets**
- See list of all your timesheets
- Status shows: Pending, Approved, Paid

**Edit Before Submitting**:
1. Open timesheet
2. Click **Edit** (only if not yet submitted)
3. Change entries as needed
4. Click **Save**
5. Submit again

**After Approval** (Cannot edit):
- Changes must be requested from manager
- Manager can make adjustments
- Or request new timesheet for corrections

## Viewing Your Time

### View Today's Hours

**Go to**:
1. **Dashboard** → Look at **Time Worked Today**
2. Shows total hours clocked so far
3. Shows current status (Clocked In/Out)

### View Weekly Hours

**Go to**:
1. **Time** → **Weekly Time**
2. See all hours for current week
3. See daily breakdown
4. See total hours

### View Monthly Summary

**Go to**:
1. **Time** → **Monthly Report**
2. Choose month
3. See total hours that month
4. Compare to expected hours
5. See patterns

### View All Time Entries

**Go to**:
1. **Time** → **Time Entries**
2. See all your clock entries
3. Sorted by date
4. Shows clock in/out times
5. Shows duration

## Editing Time Entries

### Correct a Time Entry

**If you entered wrong time**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Time Entries**
2. Find the entry to edit
3. Click **Edit**
4. Change the time
5. Click **Save**

**Note**: Can only edit entries not yet in a submitted timesheet.

### Add a Missing Entry

**If you forgot to clock in/out**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Time Entries**
2. Click **Add Manual Entry**
3. Enter:
   - Date
   - Start time
   - End time
   - Reason (e.g., "Forgot to clock in")
4. Click **Add**

**Note**: Notify your manager when adding manual entries.

## Time Approval

### Manager Approval

Your manager reviews your timesheet:
- Checks all entries are correct
- Verifies against their records
- Approves or requests changes

**If approved**: Goes to payroll ✅

**If rejected**: You'll see notification to fix issues

### Payroll Processing

After manager approval:
1. Timesheet sent to payroll
2. Payroll calculates pay
3. You receive payslip
4. Payment processed
5. You can view payslip in **Pay** section

## Tips & Best Practices

✅ **DO**:
- Clock in when you arrive
- Clock out when you leave
- Clock in after breaks
- Submit timesheet on time
- Review for accuracy before submitting
- Note any manual entries
- Notify manager of discrepancies

❌ **DON'T**:
- Share your login to have someone else clock in
- Clock in before you arrive
- Forget to clock out (leads to inaccurate data)
- Submit wrong timesheet
- Alter times dishonestly
- Ignore manager feedback

## Troubleshooting

### Clock In Button Not Working
- Refresh page
- Check you're not already clocked in
- Try different browser
- Contact support

### Wrong Time Showing
- Check your browser time is correct
- Check timezone setting
- Edit the entry if just made
- Contact manager if in past timesheet

### Timesheet Won't Submit
- All entries verified?
- No validation errors?
- Try refreshing page
- Contact support

## FAQ

**Q: What if I'm sick?**
A: Use leave request. Don't clock in. Notify manager same day.

**Q: What if I work from home?**
A: Still clock in/out same way. Location doesn't matter.

**Q: Can I clock in early?**
A: Usually no (depends on policy). Ask your manager.

**Q: What if system is down?**
A: Contact IT. Manual timesheet may be created.

**Q: Can I change submitted timesheet?**
A: No, but manager can. Request changes from manager.
