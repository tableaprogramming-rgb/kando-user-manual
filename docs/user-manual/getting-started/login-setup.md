# Login & Setup Guide

This guide walks you through logging into Kando and setting up your account for the first time.

## Before You Start

You should have received:
- **Kando System URL** - Link to access Kando
- **Username** - Your login ID (usually employee ID or email)
- **Temporary Password** - Initial password to login

If you haven't received these, contact your HR department.

## Logging In

### Step 1: Visit the Kando URL
Open your web browser and go to the URL provided by your HR team.

*Example: `https://kando.yourcompany.com`*

You should see the **Kando Login Page**.

### Step 2: Enter Your Credentials
1. **Username**: Type your employee ID or email
2. **Password**: Type your temporary password provided by HR
3. Click **Login** or press Enter

### Step 3: First Login - Change Password
On your first login, Kando will ask you to change your password.

1. **Old Password**: Enter the temporary password
2. **New Password**: Kando doesn't enforce a minimum length or character mix, but for your own security we recommend:
   - At least 8 characters
   - Mix of uppercase and lowercase
   - Include numbers and symbols
   - Don't use your name or employee ID
3. **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your new password (must match exactly)
4. Click **Update Password**

**Remember your new password!** You'll need it every time you login.

### Step 4: Login Again
Login with your new password:
1. **Username**: Your employee ID or email
2. **Password**: Your new password
3. Click **Login**

## Account Setup

After logging in for the first time, complete your profile setup.

### Step 1: Personal Information
Update your basic information:
- **Full Name** - Usually pre-filled
- **Email** - Your work email
- **Phone** - Your contact number
- **Department** - Your team/division
- **Manager** - Your direct supervisor

Click **Save** to continue.

### Step 2: Bank Details (For Payroll)
If processing payroll, add:
- **Account Holder Name**
- **Account Number**
- **Routing Number** (for US) or equivalent
- **Bank Name**
- **Account Type** - Checking or Savings

Click **Save** to continue.

*Note: This information is encrypted and secure.*

### Step 3: Notification Preferences
Choose how you want to receive notifications:
- **Email Notifications** - Important updates via email
- **In-App Notifications** - Alerts within Kando
- **Frequency** - How often to receive digest emails

Click **Save** to continue.

### Step 4: Verify Your Setup
Review the information you entered:
- Personal information correct?
- Bank details accurate?
- Notification preferences set?

Click **Complete Setup** when everything looks good.

## Your Dashboard

After setup, you're taken to your **Dashboard** - your personal home page in Kando.

**Navigation Menu** (Left side)

## Changing Your Password

If you want to change your password later:

1. Click **Settings** → **Security** (or **Profile**)
2. Click **Change Password**
3. Enter:
   - **Current Password** - Your existing password
   - **New Password** - Your new password
   - **Confirm Password** - Repeat your new password
4. Click **Change Password**

## Logging Out

Always logout when done, especially on shared computers:

1. Click your **Profile Icon** (top-right corner)
2. Click **Logout**
3. Confirm you want to logout

You're now logged out. Close the browser window to be safe.

## First Time Tips

✅ **DO**:
- Write down your new password in a safe place
- Complete all setup steps
- Add a phone number for recovery
- Test the system with a small task
- Set your notification preferences
- Take a screenshot of your dashboard

❌ **DON'T**:
- Share your password with anyone
- Leave yourself logged in on public computers
- Use a simple password (123456, password, etc.)
- Skip the bank details if you're paid by direct deposit
- Close the browser without logging out

## Common Login Issues

### "Invalid Username or Password"
- Check you entered your username correctly
- Verify caps lock isn't on
- Ensure password is spelled correctly
- Try again

### "Account Locked"
- Too many failed login attempts
- Wait 15 minutes and try again, OR
- Click **Forgot password?** to reset
- Contact IT if still locked

### "I Forgot My Password"
1. On the login page, click **Reset** next to "Forgot password?"
2. Enter your **Email Address** (not your username) and click **Reset Password**
3. You'll see a confirmation: *"Kindly check your email. We have sent you a reset password link on your registered email address."*
4. Open the email and click **Reset Password**
   - **The link expires in 60 minutes.** If it expires, you'll see a "Reset Link Expired" screen — click **Request Another Reset** to get a new one.
   - If no email arrives, check your spam folder. If your email address isn't on file, you won't receive one — contact HR/IT to confirm it.
5. On the **Set a New Password** page, enter and confirm your **New Password**, then submit
6. You'll see: *"Your password has been successfully updated."* Login with your new password.

**Note**: The mobile app doesn't have a "Forgot password?" option yet. If you're locked out and only have the mobile app, open a browser and use the web login page to reset your password — then log back into the mobile app with your new password.

### "Browser Error / Won't Load"
- Refresh the page (F5)
- Try a different browser
- Clear your browser cache
- Check your internet connection
- Try again in a few minutes

## Security Tips

### Keep Your Account Secure

✅ **DO**:
- Use a strong, unique password
- Never share your login credentials
- Logout on public computers
- Report suspicious activity to IT
- Update your contact information
- Use MFA (multi-factor authentication) if available

❌ **DON'T**:
- Write password on sticky notes
- Use same password on multiple sites
- Let others use your account
- Click suspicious email links
- Share account details over email
- Ignore security warnings
