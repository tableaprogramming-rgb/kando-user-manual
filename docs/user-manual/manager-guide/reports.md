# Reports

*Generate team reports for analysis, planning, and decision-making in Kando.*

## Overview

The Reports feature allows you to:
- Generate comprehensive team performance reports
- Analyze attendance and time tracking data
- Review payroll and compensation information
- Monitor compliance with labor regulations
- Identify trends and patterns
- Export reports for presentations and sharing
- Compare performance across time periods
- Make data-driven decisions
- Prepare reports for management review
- Document team metrics for audits

Reports provide insights into team operations, helping you identify issues, plan improvements, and make informed decisions.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Access Reports

**From Dashboard**:
1. Go to **Dashboard**
2. Look for **Quick Reports** widget
3. See recent reports
4. Click **View All Reports**

**From Main Menu**:
1. Navigate to **Reports** in left sidebar
2. Or use keyboard shortcut: `Ctrl+Shift+R`
3. Choose report type

### Step 2: Select Report Parameters

**Standard Options**:
1. **Report Type**: Choose from available types
2. **Date Range**: Select start and end dates
   - Preset options: This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Year-to-Date
   - Custom: Select specific dates
3. **Team Members**: All or specific people
4. **Format**: PDF or Excel
5. Click **Generate**

**Example**:
```
Generate Report:
├─ Type: Attendance Report
├─ Date Range: Feb 1-28, 2026
├─ Team Members: All (12 people)
├─ Format: PDF
└─ Action: Generate
```

### Step 3: Review Report

**Typical Report Sections**:
1. **Summary**: High-level metrics
2. **Detailed Data**: Individual/shift breakdown
3. **Analysis**: Trends and observations
4. **Recommendations**: Suggested actions
5. **Appendix**: Supporting data

## Common Reporting Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Monthly Attendance Review

**Process**:
1. Generate **Attendance Report** for Feb 1-28
2. Review each employee:
   - Attendance rate (target: ≥95%)
   - Late arrivals (acceptable: &lt;1 per month)
   - Absences (concerning if >1 unscheduled)
3. Identify issues:
   - Chronic absentees: >2 absences/month
   - Late pattern: >2 lates/month
4. Take action:
   - Email reminder to frequent absentees
   - Schedule meeting if pattern concerning
   - Document for records
5. Share summary with leadership

### Scenario 2: Payroll Processing

**Process**:
1. End of pay period: Generate **Payroll Report**
2. Verify:
   - Hours match time tracking
   - Leave recorded correctly
   - Overtime within budget
   - No errors or discrepancies
3. Identify adjustments:
   - Time corrections needed?
   - Leave to record?
   - Adjustments to approve?
4. Approve for payroll processing
5. Send to Finance/HR for final processing

### Scenario 3: Performance Evaluation

**Quarterly Process**:
1. Generate **Performance Report** for Q1
2. Review individual performance:
   - Goal achievement rate
   - Attendance/reliability
   - Quality metrics
   - Teamwork/collaboration

   - Recognize in team meeting
   - Consider for bonus/promotion
4. Identify concerns:
   - Performance gaps
   - Need for coaching/training
   - Improvement plans

## Report Analysis & Interpretation

### Understanding Metrics

**Attendance Metrics**:
- **Attendance Rate**: Percentage of scheduled days worked
  - Formula: Days Present / Days Scheduled × 100
  - Target: ≥95%
  - Benchmark: Industry avg. 94-96%

- **Punctuality Rate**: Percentage of arrivals on time
  - Formula: On-Time Days / Days Worked × 100
  - Target: ≥98%
  - Benchmark: Industry avg. 96-98%

- **Absence Rate**: Unscheduled absences
  - Formula: Unscheduled Absences / Days Scheduled × 100
  - Target: &lt;5%
  - Benchmark: Industry avg. 3-5%

**Performance Metrics**:
- **Productivity Rate**: Work output vs. target
  - Formula: Actual Output / Target Output × 100
  - Target: 90-110% (100% is target)
  - Below 85%: Underperforming
  - Above 115%: Overworking (risk)

- **Error Rate**: Quality issues
  - Formula: Errors / Total Tasks × 100
  - Target: &lt;3%
  - Benchmark: Varies by role

**Payroll Metrics**:
- **Labor Cost %**: Payroll as % of revenue
  - Formula: Total Payroll / Revenue × 100
  - Target: 20-35% (varies by industry)
  - Over 40%: Cost concern
  - Under 15%: May indicate understaffing

- **Overtime Rate**: Percentage of hours worked beyond standard
  - Formula: Overtime Hours / Total Hours × 100
  - Target: &lt;10%
  - Benchmark: Industry avg. 5-15%

### Identifying Trends

**Positive Trends**:
- Improving attendance rate (↑)
- Decreasing absenteeism (↓)
- Improving on-time performance (↑)
- Increasing productivity (↑)
- Decreasing error rate (↓)
- Declining overtime (↓)

**Concerning Trends**:
- Declining attendance (↓)
- Increasing absences (↑)
- Increasing late arrivals (↑)
- Declining productivity (↓)
- Increasing errors (↑)
- Increasing overtime (↑)
- Increasing leave usage (↑)

**Action Triggers**:
```
If Attendance < 90%:
→ Immediate action required
→ Schedule meeting with employee
→ Develop improvement plan
→ Consider disciplinary action

If Productivity < 75%:
→ Training needed
→ Workload assessment
→ Performance improvement plan
→ Additional support or resources

If Errors > 5%:
→ Quality review
→ Training reinforcement
→ Process improvement
→ Support or mentoring
```

## Report Limitations & Cautions

### Organization & Data Scope

Reports only include data from your own organization — you won't see records for employees, teams, or compensation belonging to a different organization, even if you know their name or ID. This is intentional: it keeps payroll and compliance data private between organizations sharing the same Kando instance.

If a report seems to be missing someone you expect to see, confirm they're actually part of your organization rather than a related one.

### Data Accuracy

**Reports reflect**:
- Only data entered in Kando
- Depends on accuracy of time clock
- Relies on employee accuracy
- Only shows approved transactions

**Reports DO NOT show**:
- Informal productivity (conversations, brainstorming)
- Quality aspects not captured in data
- Employee effort or attitude
- External factors affecting performance

### Using Reports Responsibly

**Best Practices**:
- ✅ Use data to support decisions, not sole basis
- ✅ Verify data before acting on it
- ✅ Consider context (why metrics are what they are)
- ✅ Discuss concerns with employee (listen to perspective)
- ✅ Use for improvement, not punishment
- ❌ Don't compare employees in public using reports
- ❌ Don't make final decisions based on single month
- ❌ Don't ignore context (busy season, emergencies)
- ❌ Don't use reports to justify predetermined decisions

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: "Access Denied" or "Not Found" when viewing a compensation/compliance record from a report
**Cause**: The record belongs to a different organization, or you don't have the required permission
**Solution**:
1. Confirm the employee/record is part of your organization
2. Verify you have the necessary payroll/compliance permission (ask your Owner or HR Admin)
3. This is expected behavior — Kando doesn't allow cross-organization access, even accidentally

**Problem**: "Report shows incomplete data"
**Cause**: Data not entered yet, or employee hasn't clocked in
**Solution**:
1. Verify date range is correct
2. Check if all employees clocked in for period
3. Ensure leave/adjustments were approved
4. Try generating again after data entries complete
5. Contact support if data missing for completed period

**Problem**: "Report won't generate - error message"
**Cause**: System error, large date range, or permission issue
**Solution**:
1. Refresh page and try again
2. Try smaller date range (1 month instead of 1 year)
3. Export as Excel instead of PDF (sometimes faster)
4. Verify you have permission to generate reports
5. Contact support if persists

**Problem**: "Exported report looks different than preview"
**Cause**: Formatting differences in PDF/Excel export
**Solution**:
1. Expected - formatting may vary by viewer
2. PDF should be most consistent
3. Try printing PDF to see true format
4. Use Excel if need editable version

**Problem**: "Can't find report I generated yesterday"
**Cause**: Reports not saved automatically; need to regenerate or download
**Solution**:
1. Reports are typically temporary (shown in browser)
2. Download immediately after generating
3. Or create scheduled report for automatic generation
4. Check email if created as scheduled report
5. All reports can be regenerated anytime

## Best Practices for Reporting

✅ **DO**:
- **Generate reports regularly** (at least monthly)
- **Act on insights** (don't just generate and ignore)
- **Verify data** before making decisions
- **Consider context** (busy season, new hires, etc.)
- **Share appropriate data** (don't overshare sensitive info)
- **Archive reports** for audit trail
- **Use for improvement** (support and development)
- **Compare trends** (month-to-month, year-over-year)
- **Schedule recurring reports** (automation saves time)
- **Discuss findings** with team (transparency builds trust)

❌ **DON'T**:
- Generate reports just to file (actually use them)
- Use single data point to make major decisions
- Compare employees publicly using data
- Use reports punitively (use for support/improvement)
- Ignore concerning trends (address proactively)
- Share sensitive data unnecessarily
- Forget to verify data accuracy
- Make assumptions without investigation
- Delay action when trends concerning
- Use reports without considering context

### Predictive Analytics

**What You Can Discover**:
```
Predictive Insights Available:
├─ Overtime Trends: Forecasted OT needs based on historical patterns
├─ Absence Patterns: Predicted absence clustering (Mondays, Fridays, sick leave)
```

**How to Access**:

2. Select **Predictive Models**
3. Choose timeframe (next 30/90/180 days)
4. View predicted trends with confidence intervals
5. Export forecast data for planning

**Real-World Example**:
> Sales team lead sees "High OT Risk" forecast for March. System predicts 180+ OT hours based on sales cycle and historical March patterns. She proactively schedules additional staffing and adjusts workload to prevent burnout.

### Custom Report Builder

**Creating Your Own Reports**:
1. Go to **Reports** → **Custom Reports**
2. Click **Create New Report**
3. Select data source (Timekeeping, Payroll, Approvals, Attendance)
4. Define columns:
   - Drag fields from Available to Selected
   - Adjust order by dragging
   - Set filters (date range, department, employee type)
5. Configure calculations:
   - Aggregate (sum, average, count, distinct count)
   - Group by (employee, week, month, department)
   - Sort order
6. Set formatting:
   - Number format (decimal places, currency)
   - Date format (MM/DD/YYYY, DD-MM-YYYY, etc.)
7. Name report and click **Save**

**Advanced Filters**:
- **Date Ranges**: Fixed (last 30 days) or Rolling (last 30 days always)
- **Complex Conditions**: AND/OR combinations (e.g., "OT > 5 AND Attendance < 90%")
- **Calculated Fields**: Create formulas (e.g., "Net Hours = Total - Absence")
- **Conditional Formatting**: Highlight values exceeding thresholds

**Example Custom Reports**:
```
Manager Development Insights:
├─ "Team Utilization Report"
│  ├─ Shows: Hours worked vs allocated per employee
│  ├─ Filters: Current month, active employees
│  └─ Identifies: Underutilized resources
│
├─ "Leave Forecasting"
│  ├─ Shows: Approved leaves by month (next 6 months)
│  ├─ Identifies: Coverage gaps in advance
│  └─ Helps: Schedule backfill or redistribute work
│
└─ "Performance Correlation"
   ├─ Shows: Absence vs performance ratings
   ├─ Identifies: Attendance-performance relationships
   └─ Helps: Understand context for performance discussions
```

### Benchmarking and Comparisons

**Compare Your Team Against Standards**:
1. Go to **Reports** → **Benchmarking**
2. Select metric (Overtime, Attendance, Turnaround Time on Approvals)
3. Choose comparison:
   - **Your Team vs. Org Average**: How does your team compare company-wide?
   - **Your Team vs. Similar Teams**: Compare against teams of similar size/type
   - **Year-over-Year**: Your team this year vs. last year same period
   - **Monthly Trend**: How are metrics changing month-to-month?
4. View visual comparison (bar charts, heat maps)
5. Drill down into specific employees or periods

**Benchmarking Insights**:
```
Example: Overtime Benchmarking

Your Team Average: 18 OT hours/employee/month
Status: 150% of average (RED FLAG)

Actions to Take:
├─ Investigate root cause (understaffed, peak season, workload distribution)
```

### Compliance Reporting

**Legal and Policy Compliance Tracking**:

**What Compliance Reports Cover**:
- **Rest Day Compliance**: Ensuring minimum 2 days/week (or per policy)
- **Break Requirements**: Employees getting required break time
- **Working Hours Limits**: Not exceeding maximum weekly hours
- **Overtime Limits**: Respecting policy caps and rest requirements
- **Leave Entitlements**: Using correct leave types, respecting allocations
- **Notification Requirements**: Absences reported within policy timelines
- **Documentation**: Medical certificates, approval documentation present

**Generating Compliance Reports**:
1. Go to **Reports** → **Compliance**

3. Choose period (monthly, quarterly, annual)
4. View:
   - **Summary**: % of employees compliant
   - **Exceptions**: Employees with policy violations
   - **Severity**: High-risk violations vs. minor issues
   - **Details**: Specific violations by employee
5. Export for HR records

**Taking Corrective Action**:
```
Compliance Issue: "Employee X - Rest Day Violation"
```
