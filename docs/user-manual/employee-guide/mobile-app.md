# Kando Mobile App

*Clock in, submit requests, and approve your team from your phone.*

## Overview

The Kando mobile app covers most of what you can do on the web: clock in/out, submit leave and other requests, check your leave balance, and — if you're a manager — approve requests from your team. Anything you do on mobile shows up on web instantly, and vice versa.

This page covers installing the app, logging in, and what's available on mobile. For full step-by-step instructions on any feature, see the linked guide — the web instructions apply on mobile too, just with tapping instead of clicking.

## Installing & Logging In

### Step 1: Download the App

Search for **Kando** in the Apple App Store (iPhone/iPad) or Google Play Store (Android) and install it.

### Step 2: Enter Your Organization Domain

1. Open the app
2. Enter your **Organization domain** — this is the short name from your company's Kando web address. If your team logs into `https://acme.kando.app`, your domain is `acme`.
3. Tap **Continue**

Don't know your domain? Ask your HR team.

### Step 3: Log In

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password** — the same credentials you use on web
2. Tap **Login**

### Step 4: Allow Location Access

The app will ask for location access: *"Kando needs your location to record accurate time-in and time-out details."* Tap **Enable Location** so your clock in/out times record correctly.

## What You Can Do on Mobile

### Time Tracking

Clock in and out from the **Home** screen, same as web. Your timer syncs in real time — if you clock in on mobile, it appears on your web dashboard instantly, and vice versa. See [Time Tracking Guide](time-tracking.md) for the full picture, including manual entries and timesheets.

### Submitting Requests

From the **Requests** tab → **My Requests**, tap **+** to submit any of these:

| Request Type | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Leave** | Vacation, sick, personal, or any leave type your organization has configured |
| **Overtime** | Requesting extra hours |
| **Manual Timelog** | Logging a missed clock in/out |
| **Schedule** | Requesting a schedule change |
| **Holiday Swap** | Swapping a holiday |

You can attach a file (photo, PDF, etc.) to any request — useful for medical certificates or evidence for a manual timelog.

Full instructions for leave and schedule requests: [Leave Management](leave-management.md), [View Schedule](view-schedule.md).

### Viewing Leave Balance History

Go to **Leaves** → **Balance History** to see your leave balance changes over time, filterable by leave type.

### Approving Requests (Managers)

If you can approve requests, you'll see a second tab: **My Approvals**. Tap any request to review it and approve or reject — you'll also see an **Approved By** section showing who's signed off so far if the request needs more than one approval. You can search My Approvals by employee name or request details.

See [Approving Requests](../manager-guide/approving-requests.md) for the full approval workflow (web instructions apply on mobile too).

### Real-Time Updates

Changes sync instantly between mobile and web while both are open and connected — no need to refresh. This includes clock in/out, and request status changes.

**Good to know**: Kando doesn't send phone notifications when the app is closed. If you want to see the latest status on a request, open the app — it'll be up to date. It won't alert you in the background.

## Mobile Limitations

A few things are web-only for now:

- **Payslips** — not available on mobile yet. View your payslip on web under [Payslip & Compensation](payslip-compensation.md)
- **Password reset** — there's no "Forgot password?" option in the app. Reset your password on web, then log back into the app
- **Changing your password / editing your profile** — not available on mobile; do this on web under [Login & Setup](../getting-started/login-setup.md)
- **Offline use** — the app needs an internet connection; there's no offline mode
- **Detailed approval filters** — mobile's My Approvals only has a search box; for filtering by date or request type, use web

## Tips & Common Issues

✅ **DO**:
- Keep location access enabled so clock in/out times are accurate
- Attach documentation to requests when required (medical certificates, etc.)
- Open the app periodically to check request status, since there's no background notification
- Use the same email/password as your web account — there's no separate mobile account

❌ **DON'T**:
- Don't expect a push notification when a request is approved/rejected — check the app
- Don't rely on the app while offline — you need a connection to clock in/out or submit requests
- Don't forget your Organization domain — without it, login will fail even with correct email/password

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In
- Double check your **Organization domain** matches your company's Kando web address
- Verify email and password are correct (same as web)
- Forgot your password? The app doesn't have a reset option — open a browser, reset it on the web login page, then come back and log into the app with your new password
- Contact HR if you're not sure of your organization domain

### Clock In/Out Button Not Working
- Make sure **Location Access** is enabled for Kando in your phone's Settings
- Check you have an internet connection
- Try closing and reopening the app

### My Timer Shows Differently on Phone vs. Web
- This is usually a brief network delay — both devices sync automatically within a few seconds
- Check your internet connection on both devices
- If it doesn't resolve within a minute, refresh the web page or reopen the app

### Don't See a Request I Just Submitted
- Real-time sync requires an internet connection on both devices — check yours
- Reopen the app to force a refresh
- Confirm the request actually submitted (check **My Requests**)
