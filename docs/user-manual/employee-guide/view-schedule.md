# View Schedule

*Understand your work schedule, shifts, and availability in Kando.*

## Overview

The Schedule feature allows you to:
- View your assigned shifts and working hours
- See your roster for the week, month, or custom period
- Check team members' schedules (if authorized)
- Understand different shift types (standard, flexible, rotating, etc.)
- Find out about shift changes and schedule updates
- Request shift swaps or changes
- Export your schedule for personal reference
- Receive notifications about schedule changes

Your schedule is determined by your manager based on business needs, your preferences, and leave requests. It appears in Kando as your source of truth for working hours.

## Schedule Types

Kando supports various scheduling models:

### Fixed Schedule
- Same working hours every day
- Standard 9 AM - 5 PM (or similar)
- Predictable and stable
- Most common for office-based employees

**Example**:
```
Monday-Friday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Saturday-Sunday: Off
Break: 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM
```

### Rotating Shifts
- Different shifts on different days
- Morning, afternoon, or night shifts
- Rotates weekly or monthly
- Common for 24/7 operations

**Example**:
```
Week 1: Morning shift (6 AM - 2 PM)
Week 2: Afternoon shift (2 PM - 10 PM)
Week 3: Night shift (10 PM - 6 AM)
```

### Flexible Schedule
- Flexible start/end times within window
- Core hours when present required (e.g., 10 AM - 3 PM)
- Choose arrival and departure times
- Allows work-life balance

**Example**:
```
Core Hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (must be present)
Flexible Window: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM (choose arrival)
Flexible Window: 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM (choose departure)
```

### On-Call Schedule
- May be called in as needed
- Availability requirements specified
- Stand-by or emergency response
- Common for support/emergency roles

### Compressed Work Week
- Work same hours in fewer days
- Example: 40 hours in 4 days
- Longer daily hours, extra day off
- Improves work-life balance

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Access Your Schedule

**From Dashboard**:
1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Look for **Schedule** widget
3. See next 7 days of your schedule
4. Click **View Full Schedule**

**From Main Menu**:
1. Navigate to **Schedule** in left sidebar
2. Or use keyboard shortcut: `Ctrl+Shift+S`
3. Default view shows current week

### Step 2: Navigate Your Schedule

**Week View** (Default):
- Shows Monday-Sunday
- Current week highlighted
- Navigate arrows to move between weeks
- Today marked with blue circle

**Month View**:
1. Click **Month** button
2. See entire month at once
3. Color-coded shifts
4. Click dates for detailed view

**Day View**:
1. Click on any day
2. See detailed breakdown:
   - Start time, end time
   - Break duration
   - Total working hours
   - Any notes from manager
   - Shift type

### Step 3: Understand Your Shift

Look for these details in your schedule:

- **Date & Day**: When your shift is scheduled
- **Start Time**: When you should arrive/log in
- **End Time**: When you should leave/log out
- **Break Duration**: Unpaid break time (typically 1 hour)
- **Total Hours**: Working hours (excluding breaks)
- **Shift Type**: Standard, Flexible, On-call, etc.
- **Location**: Where to work (office, remote, hybrid)
- **Notes**: Any special instructions from manager

## Common Tasks

### View Next Week's Schedule

1. Navigate to **Schedule**
2. Current week displayed by default
3. Click **Right arrow** (→) to move forward
4. See next week's shifts and dates
5. Plan accordingly for the week

**Keyboard Shortcut**: `→` key (when schedule is focused)

**Common Use**: Plan time off, coordinate personal activities, check days off

### Check Your Working Hours

1. Open **Schedule**
2. Hover over any date to see:
   - Start time
   - End time
   - Total hours
   - Break time
3. Or click date for full details

**Example Display**:
```
Monday, Feb 17, 2026
9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
8 hours (1 hour break)
Location: Office, Building A
```

### Export Your Schedule

1. Navigate to **Schedule**
2. Select date range:
   - Click **Date Range** filter
   - Choose start and end dates
   - Or select preset (This Week, This Month)
3. Click **Export** button
4. Choose format:
   - **PDF**: Print-friendly format
   - **ICS**: Import into calendar (Outlook, Google Calendar)
   - **Excel**: Spreadsheet format
5. File downloads to your computer

**Using ICS with Google Calendar**:
1. Export as ICS file
2. Go to Google Calendar
3. Click Settings → Import & Export
4. Upload ICS file
5. Shifts appear automatically in your calendar

### Request a Shift Swap

**Scenario**: Your shift on Thursday conflicts with an appointment, and you want to swap with a colleague

1. Navigate to **Schedule**
2. Find the shift you want to change (Thursday)
3. Click **Request Swap**
4. **New Date**: Select when you want to shift instead
5. **Reason**: "Medical appointment on Thursday"
6. **With Colleague**: Select colleague (optional, system finds available people)
7. **Swap Partner**: System suggests colleagues working that day
8. Click **Send Request**

**Important**: Both parties and manager must approve

**📱 Also on mobile**: Submit schedule change requests and holiday swap requests from the Kando mobile app — see [Kando Mobile App](mobile-app.md).

### Update Personal Availability

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Schedule Preferences**
2. Set preferred working hours if flexible:
   - **Preferred Start Time**
   - **Preferred End Time**
   - **Preferred Days Off**
3. Specify restrictions:
   - Days unavailable
   - Times unavailable
   - Shift preferences
4. Click **Save Preferences**

**Note**: Preferences are suggestions; manager has final scheduling authority

### View Schedule With Leave

1. Open **Schedule**
2. Approved leave shows as different color:
   - 🔵 Blue = Vacation Leave
   - 🟠 Orange = Sick Leave
   - On those days, no regular shift displayed
3. Filter view:
   - Click **Show Leave** toggle
   - Include/exclude leave from view

**Example View**:
```
Monday: 9 AM - 5 PM (Working)
Tuesday: VACATION (Approved leave)
Wednesday: 9 AM - 5 PM (Working)
```

### Find Your Days Off

1. Open **Schedule**
2. Look for blank spaces or "OFF" label
3. Click on off day for details:
   - Is it a weekend? (Regular off)
   - Is it requested? (Vacation/leave)
   - Is it a company holiday?
4. View upcoming off days:
   - Month view easier to spot
   - Export to calendar for reference

## Tips & Tricks

✅ **DO**:
- **Check schedule weekly** for any changes or updates
- **Plan personal activities** around scheduled off days
- **Export schedule to personal calendar** for easier tracking
- **Review break times** to plan meals and rest
- **Notify manager immediately** if you can't make a shift
- **Request swaps early** rather than last-minute
- **Check for company holidays** to avoid confusion
- **Use reminders** on your personal calendar for early shifts
- **Keep backup plans** for unexpected schedule changes
- **Communicate with team** about shift coverage

❌ **DON'T**:
- Assume schedule won't change (always check for updates)
- Request unreasonable shift swaps without valid reason
- Work beyond your scheduled hours without approval
- Ignore notifications about schedule changes
- Forget to clock out at end of shift
- Leave shifts uncovered without swapping
- Assume your manager knows you need a specific day off
- Change your schedule without manager approval
- Rely only on memory; always verify in Kando
- Request swaps during busy periods without discussion

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often is my schedule updated?**
A: Schedules are typically finalized weekly or bi-weekly. Check Kando for latest updates. Your manager will notify you of significant changes.

**Q: Can I request my preferred schedule?**
A: Yes, discuss preferences with your manager. Use **Schedule Preferences** to set availability. Manager considers requests based on business needs.

**Q: What if there's an error in my schedule?**
A: Contact your manager immediately. Don't work the wrong hours; inform your manager so it can be corrected and recorded in your timesheet.

**Q: Can I see my team's schedule?**
A: Yes, if permissions allow. Navigate to **Team Schedule** to see colleagues' shifts. Useful for coordination and coverage planning.

**Q: What does "Flexible Shift" mean?**
A: You can choose your start/end times within defined windows. Core hours are when you must be present. Communicate daily with your team about your schedule.

**Q: How far in advance is schedule published?**
A: Most organizations publish 2-4 weeks in advance. Check company policy. Emergency changes may be made with less notice.

**Q: What if I need a permanent schedule change?**
A: Discuss with your manager to request a schedule change. Submit formal request with reason. Changes typically take effect in next scheduling cycle.

**Q: Can I work overtime not on my schedule?**
A: Only if manager approves in advance. Notify manager before working extra hours. Use Kando to log actual hours, not just scheduled hours.

**Q: How is my shift affected by leave?**
A: Approved leave replaces your scheduled shift. On leave days, you don't clock in. Your timesheet shows leave instead of regular hours.

**Q: What if the schedule conflicts with my leave?**
A: Approved leave takes precedence. Your manager will adjust schedule or sync leave dates with schedule.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: "My schedule doesn't match what my manager told me"
**Cause**: Schedule not updated in system, or Kando displaying cached data
**Solution**:
1. Refresh page (Ctrl+R) to reload latest schedule
2. Check if update notification is pending
3. Confirm with manager if schedule was recently changed
4. Verify you're looking at correct time period
5. Contact HR if discrepancy persists

**Problem**: "I can't see next month's schedule"
**Cause**: Schedule not yet published for that period
**Solution**:
1. Check when schedule is typically published (weekly/bi-weekly)
2. Confirm with manager when next schedule will be available
3. Ask manager for tentative schedule if needed urgently
4. Set reminder to check schedule on publication date

**Problem**: "The schedule shows wrong start time"
**Cause**: System error, timezone issue, or recent change not reflected
**Solution**:
1. Verify timezone in **Settings** → **Preferences**
2. Refresh page and try again
3. Compare with paper schedule or manager's email
4. Contact manager to confirm correct time
5. Request correction if error confirmed

**Problem**: "Export to calendar didn't work"
**Cause**: Browser issue, file format incompatibility, or incorrect import
**Solution**:
1. Try different export format (ICS, PDF, Excel)
2. Download file and verify it opened correctly
3. Use different calendar application to import
4. Ensure calendar app is up to date
5. Contact support if issue persists

**Problem**: "Shift times changed without notification"
**Cause**: Manager updated schedule, notification not received
**Solution**:
1. Check notification preferences in **Settings**
2. Verify email notifications are enabled
3. Check email spam folder for notifications
4. Contact manager to confirm change details
5. Update your personal calendar with new times

**Problem**: "Can't request a shift swap"
**Cause**: System restrictions, insufficient time before shift, or policy
**Solution**:
1. Swap requests usually need 48 hours notice
2. Confirm shift is not already covered by someone
3. Check if swap is allowed for your shift type
4. Contact manager for manual swap request if urgent
5. Try swapping with specific colleague rather than system auto-matching

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Regular 9-5 Fixed Schedule
**Your Schedule**:
- Monday-Friday: 9 AM - 5 PM
- Saturday-Sunday: Off
- Break: 12 PM - 1 PM

**What to Know**:
- Total working hours: 8 hours/day = 40 hours/week
- Same schedule every week (unless requesting change)
- Overtime only if working beyond 5 PM
- Leave replaces your 8-hour shift

**Common Task**: Check schedule before approving meetings outside 9-5

### Scenario 2: Rotating Shift Pattern
**Your Schedule** (Example):
- Week 1: Morning (6 AM - 2 PM)
- Week 2: Afternoon (2 PM - 10 PM)
- Week 3: Night (10 PM - 6 AM)
- Then repeats

**What to Know**:
- Schedule changes weekly
- Check calendar frequently for shift changes
- Plan personal activities around shift rotations
- Ensure adequate rest between different shifts
- May be difficult to request swaps due to coverage needs

**Common Task**: Export 3-month schedule to plan personal calendar

### Scenario 3: Flexible Work Hours
**Your Schedule**:
- Core Hours: 10 AM - 3 PM (must be present)
- Flexible Arrival: 7 AM - 10 AM (choose time)
- Flexible Departure: 3 PM - 6 PM (choose time)
- Total: 8 hours required

**What to Know**:
- You have flexibility in when you start/end
- But must be present during core hours
- Communicate daily with team about your schedule
- Useful for commuting flexibility or family needs
- Same 8 hours/day required as fixed schedule

**Common Task**: Inform team in morning standup of your arrival time

### Scenario 4: Schedule Change Notification
**Situation**: Manager updates your schedule, you receive notification

**Steps**:
1. Receive notification: "Your schedule has been updated"
2. Check Kando **Schedule** page
3. Review changes:
   - New shift times?
   - Different days?
   - Location changes?
4. Contact manager if questions
5. Update personal calendar with new times
6. Plan for any work-life impact

**Timeline**: Usually effective 1-2 weeks after notification
