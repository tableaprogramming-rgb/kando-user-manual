# Time Tracking Guide

Learn how to track your work time, clock in and out, and submit timesheets in Kando.

## What is Time Tracking?

Time tracking records when you work and how long you work. It helps:
- Calculate your pay accurately
- Track attendance
- Identify work patterns
- Ensure accountability
- Plan scheduling

## Clock In / Clock Out

### Clocking In

**Goal**: Start tracking your work time for the day

**Steps**:
1. Click **My Time** in the left menu
2. Click **Clock In**
3. You'll see a confirmation: "Timelog created"
4. The timer starts running, and also appears next to your profile icon at the top of every page

**Note**: You need a shift scheduled for today to clock in. If you don't have one, you'll see: "You don't have a schedule for current time." Contact your manager to get a shift assigned.

**Keyboard Shortcut**: Press `Ctrl+I` to clock in instantly

### Clocking Out

**Goal**: Stop tracking your work time

**Steps**:
1. Click **My Time** in the left menu (or click the running timer at the top of the page)
2. Click **Clock Out**
3. Confirm when asked: "Are you sure you want to clock out?"
4. You'll see a confirmation: "Timelog updated"

**Keyboard Shortcut**: Press `Ctrl+O` to clock out instantly

**📱 Also on mobile**: Clock in/out works the same way in the Kando mobile app — see [Kando Mobile App](mobile-app.md).

## Multi-Device Sync

You can clock in on your phone and clock out on the web — or the other way around. Changes sync instantly between mobile and web, so both always show the same status.

**Example**: Maria clocks in on mobile at 9:00 AM in the parking lot. By the time she sits down and opens Kando on her desktop, her status already shows "Clocked In" — no refresh needed.

**Note**: This only works while both devices have an internet connection. If sync seems delayed, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting) below.

### Browser Timer Indicator

While you're clocked in, your browser tab shows a colored dot on the Kando favicon so you can tell you're on the clock without switching tabs:

- 🟢 **Green** – actively clocked in
- 🟡 **Yellow** – on break

The dot disappears when you clock out. This only appears in the web browser — the mobile app shows your timer directly in the app instead.

## Common Scenarios

### Forgot to Clock In

**Situation**: You worked all day but forgot to clock in.

**Solution**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Time Entries**
2. Click **Add Manual Entry**
3. Enter:
   - **Date**: Today
   - **Start Time**: When you arrived (e.g., 09:00 AM)
   - **End Time**: When you left (e.g., 05:00 PM)
   - **Notes**: "Manual entry - forgot to clock in"
4. Click **Add Entry**
5. Notify your manager

### Forgot to Clock Out

**Situation**: You clocked in but forgot to clock out.

**Solution**:
1. Go to **Time** → **My Clocking**
2. Find the entry that's still open (shows "Clocked In")
3. Click **Clock Out Now**
4. It will clock you out with current time
5. If wrong time, edit the entry

### Worked Break Time

**Situation**: You took a 1-hour lunch break but clocked out then back in.

**Note**: Your timesheet will show actual working hours, not including breaks.

**To add break time**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Breaks**
2. Click **Add Break**
3. Enter break duration
4. This reduces your total hours automatically

### Overtime

**Situation**: You worked extra hours beyond your normal shift.

**Handling**:
1. Clock in and out normally (captures extra time)
2. When submitting timesheet, overtime is automatically flagged
3. Manager sees overtime and approves
4. Payroll processes overtime pay

## Timesheet Submission

### What is a Timesheet?

A **timesheet** summarizes your work hours for a period (usually 1 week).

It shows:
- Daily hours worked
- Total hours for the period
- Any overtime
- Days off or leave taken
- Status (pending, approved, paid)

### Create Your Timesheet

**When**: Usually Friday or end of period

**Steps**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Timesheets**
2. Click **Create New Timesheet** (or view weekly list)
3. System shows all your clock entries for the week
4. Review each day:
   - Hours are correct?
   - Any manual entries need noting?
   - Matches what you actually worked?
5. Enter **Notes** if needed
6. Click **Submit Timesheet**

### Submit Your Timesheet

**Steps**:
1. Open your timesheet (from dashboard or Time menu)
2. Review all entries
3. Click **Submit for Approval**
4. Add comments if needed
5. Click **Submit** to confirm

**What happens next**:
- Your manager gets a notification
- They review and approve
- You get a confirmation email
- Approved timesheet goes to payroll

### Manage Your Timesheet

**View Status**:
- Go to **Time** → **Timesheets**
- See list of all your timesheets
- Status shows: Pending, Approved, Paid

**Edit Before Submitting**:
1. Open timesheet
2. Click **Edit** (only if not yet submitted)
3. Change entries as needed
4. Click **Save**
5. Submit again

**After Approval** (Cannot edit):
- Changes must be requested from manager
- Manager can make adjustments
- Or request new timesheet for corrections

## Editing Time Entries

### Correct a Time Entry

**If you entered wrong time**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Time Entries**
2. Find the entry to edit
3. Click **Edit**
4. Change the time
5. Click **Save**

**Note**: Can only edit entries not yet in a submitted timesheet.

### Add a Missing Entry

**If you forgot to clock in/out**:
1. Go to **Time** → **Time Entries**
2. Click **Add Manual Entry**
3. Enter:
   - Date
   - Start time
   - End time
   - Reason (e.g., "Forgot to clock in")
4. Click **Add**

**Note**: Notify your manager when adding manual entries.

## Time Approval

Your manager reviews your timesheet:
- Checks all entries are correct
- Verifies against their records
- Approves or requests changes

**If approved**: Goes to payroll ✅

**If rejected**: You'll see notification to fix issues

After manager approval:
1. Timesheet sent to payroll

5. You can view payslip in **Pay** section

## Understanding Timesheet Finalization

### What is a Posted/Finalized Timesheet?

After your manager approves your timesheet, it may be "**posted**" or "**finalized**" by your organization's payroll team. This means:

- ✅ Your timesheet has been approved and processed
- ✅ Your pay has been calculated based on this data
- ✅ The data is locked to prevent accidental changes
- ❌ You CAN'T modify entries for those dates
- ❌ You CAN'T clock in/out for those dates
- ❌ You CAN'T request leave for those dates

**Key Concept**: Once a timesheet is finalized, it becomes read-only to protect payroll integrity.

### Why Timesheets Get Finalized

Timesheets are finalized when:
1. **Payroll Period Closes**: Monthly/weekly payroll cutoff date passes
2. **Payroll Calculated**: HR processes pay based on timesheet
3. **Payslips Generated**: Your pay is determined from this data
4. **Payments Made**: Salary deposited to your account

**Why Lock It?**: If changes could be made after pay calculation, your pay would be incorrect and hard to track.

### The Timeline

```
Mon-Fri (Week 1): You work and clock in/out
   ↓
Fri (End of week): Manager reviews and approves timesheet
   ↓
Mon (Week 2): HR reviews all approved timesheets
   ↓
Wed (Week 2): Payroll processes salaries
   ↓
Fri (Week 2): Timesheets FINALIZED - now locked
   ↓
[Your dates are now locked - can't modify]
```

### Error: "Schedule date already has posted timesheet"

**What This Means**: You tried to clock in or request leave for a date that's already in a finalized timesheet.

**Why It Happens**:
1. Your timesheet for that week/month has been finalized by payroll
2. The system is preventing changes to avoid pay discrepancies
3. This is a safety mechanism to protect your payroll accuracy

**Common Scenarios**:
- Tried to clock in for last week (already finalized)
- Tried to request leave for a date in last month (already finalized)
- Tried to add manual entry for past finalized dates

**How to Fix - Simple Cases**:
1. **Check if within current timesheet period**: If date is in current (not-yet-finalized) week, you can modify
2. **Request different date**: If possible, request leave for dates not yet finalized
3. **Wait for next period**: If you made a mistake last period, ask manager to help resolve

**How to Fix - When Manager Help Needed** (⚠️ Rare):
If you genuinely made an error in a finalized timesheet:
1. **Talk to your manager**: Explain the error and why it needs correcting
2. **Manager submits request to HR/Payroll**: Only they can unlock finalized timesheets
3. **HR reviews and reverses if justified**: Payroll reverses the posting
4. **You make the correction**: You can now edit the dates
5. **Timesheet re-finalized**: After corrections, payroll finalizes again and may adjust your pay

**Important**:
- You CANNOT reverse a finalized timesheet yourself
- Only your manager or HR/payroll can reverse finalization
- This is intentional—protects both you and the company
- Reversals create audit trail for compliance

### Blocked Actions on Posted Timesheet Dates

Once a timesheet is finalized, these actions are blocked:

| Action | Status | Reason |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Clock in/out** | ❌ Blocked | Timesheet data is locked |
| **Add time entry** | ❌ Blocked | Cannot modify past timesheet |
| **Edit time entry** | ❌ Blocked | Would change approved payroll data |
| **Request leave** | ❌ Blocked | Would affect already-calculated pay |
| **Delete time entry** | ❌ Blocked | Would invalidate payroll |

**Example Error**:
```
You: Try to request leave for Jan 15 (last week)
System: "Schedule date already has posted timesheet"
Why: Jan 15 is in last week's timesheet, which was finalized on Jan 20
Solution: Request leave for dates in current week (not finalized yet)
```

### How to Avoid Timesheet Blocking Issues

**✅ DO**:
- **Submit timesheet ASAP** after end of period (don't wait)
- **Review for accuracy** before manager approves
- **Request leave early** (before timesheet finalizes)
- **Notify manager immediately** if you find errors during period
- **Plan ahead** for past-period issues

**❌ DON'T**:
- Don't wait until end of month to request leave for early-month dates
- Don't try to clock in for past dates after timesheet finalized
- Don't request leave during finalization dates
- Don't expect manager to reverse finalized timesheet for minor errors
- Don't make changes after approving (review carefully first)

### Leave Requests During Timesheet Period

**Important Timing**:
```
Week of Jan 15-19:
├── Mon-Fri: Work and clock in/out
├── Fri: Submit timesheet
├── Request LEAVE for Jan 15-19 BEFORE Fri finalization ✅
└── After finalization: CAN'T request leave for these dates ❌

Week of Jan 22-26:
├── Request LEAVE for Jan 22-26 anytime this week ✅
├── After timesheet finalized: CAN'T request for these dates ❌
```

**Best Practice**: Request leave for dates in current or future periods, not past periods.

### When Your Timesheet Period is "Active"

**During Active Period** (timesheet not yet finalized):
- ✅ You can clock in/out
- ✅ You can add/edit time entries
- ✅ You can request leave
- ✅ Manager can approve changes

**After Finalization** (timesheet locked):
- ❌ Can't clock in/out
- ❌ Can't add/edit entries
- ❌ Can't request leave
- ✅ Can view (read-only)
- ✅ Can request manager/HR to reverse if error exists

### Understanding "Period" Terminology

In Kando, a "**Period**" is typically:
- 1 month (most common)
- 1 week (some organizations)
- 1 biweekly cycle (some organizations)

**Your organization determines the period.** Check with HR or your manager to understand:
- When is your period start/end date?
- When does timesheet finalization happen?
- How many days can timesheets be modified after submission?

## Tips & Best Practices

✅ **DO**:
- Clock in when you arrive
- Clock out when you leave
- Clock in after breaks
- Submit timesheet on time
- Review for accuracy before submitting
- Note any manual entries
- Notify manager of discrepancies

❌ **DON'T**:
- Share your login to have someone else clock in
- Clock in before you arrive
- Forget to clock out (leads to inaccurate data)
- Submit wrong timesheet
- Alter times dishonestly
- Ignore manager feedback

## Troubleshooting

### Clock In Button Not Working
- Refresh page
- Check you're not already clocked in
- Try different browser
- Contact support

### Wrong Time Showing
- Check your browser time is correct
- Check timezone setting
- Edit the entry if just made
- Contact manager if in past timesheet

### Timesheet Won't Submit
- All entries verified?
- No validation errors?
- Try refreshing page
- Contact support

### My Timer Shows Differently on Phone vs. Web
- Timers sync in real time, but a brief network delay can happen
- Check your internet connection on both devices
- Wait a moment for sync to complete, then refresh if it still doesn't match
- This is normal behavior — the system keeps both devices in sync automatically

## FAQ

**Q: What if I'm sick?**
A: Use leave request. Don't clock in. Notify manager same day.

**Q: What if I work from home?**
A: Still clock in/out same way. Location doesn't matter.

**Q: Can I clock in early?**
A: Usually no (depends on policy). Ask your manager.

**Q: What if system is down?**
A: Contact IT. Manual timesheet may be created.

**Q: Can I change submitted timesheet?**
A: No, but manager can. Request changes from manager.
