# Leave Management

*Learn how to request, manage, and track your leave in Kando.*

## Overview

Leave Management allows you to:
- View your available leave balance and entitlements
- Request different types of leave (vacation, sick leave, personal, etc.)
- Check the status of your pending leave requests
- View your leave history and past approvals
- Understand leave policies and regulations
- Manage leave dates and approvals

Leave requests require approval from your manager based on company policies and leave availability. Once approved, the time appears on your timesheet as paid leave.

## Understanding Leave Credits

### What Are Leave Credits?

**Leave credits** are the number of leave days your company allocates to you. Think of them as a "balance" that decreases when you use leave and may increase when new leave is allocated (e.g., each year or each quarter).

**Key Concept**: You can only request leave if you have available credit for that leave type.

### How Leave Credit Works

**Example Scenario**:
```
Jan 1, 2026: HR initializes your Vacation Leave credit to 20 days
│
Jan 15, 2026: You request 5 days of vacation
├── Status: Pending approval
├── Your balance: 20 - 5 = 15 days available (pending counted separately)
│
Jan 16, 2026: Manager approves your request
├── Status: Approved
├── Your balance: 20 - 5 = 15 days available (credit deducted)
│
Feb 10, 2026: You can still request up to 15 more days this year
```

### The Leave Balance Calculation

Your balance is always calculated as:

```
Available = Total Allocated - Used - Pending Approval
```

**Breaking it down**:
- **Total Allocated**: What HR assigned you (set at beginning of year or hire date)
- **Used**: Days you've already taken and manager approved
- **Pending Approval**: Days in requests waiting for manager review
- **Available**: What you can request right now

**Important**: Pending leave counts against your balance, so you can't request the same days twice.

### When Are Credits Deducted?

**Credits are deducted when leave is APPROVED**, not when you request it:

1. **You Request Leave** → System reserves the days (shown as "Pending")
2. **Manager Reviews** → Notified of pending request
3. **Manager Approves** → Credits are permanently deducted from your balance
4. **Leave Occurs** → You're marked as on leave for those dates

**Important**: If your request is REJECTED, the reserved days are released and your balance returns to normal.

### Multiple Leave Types = Multiple Credits

Each leave type has its own separate credit:

**Example**:
```
Your Credits:
├── Vacation Leave: 20 days allocated
│   ├── Used: 5 days
│   ├── Pending: 3 days
│   └── Available: 12 days ← Can request vacation
│
├── Sick Leave: 10 days allocated
│   ├── Used: 1 day
│   ├── Available: 9 days ← Can request sick leave
│
└── Personal Leave: 3 days allocated
    ├── Used: 0 days
    └── Available: 3 days ← Can request personal leave
```

**Key Point**: Just because you have 0 vacation days doesn't mean you can't request sick leave—they're tracked separately.

### Common Credit Issues

#### ❌ "No Leave Credits Available"

**What This Means**: You tried to request leave, but you have no available credit for that leave type.

**Why It Happens**:
1. You've used all your allocated leave for this leave type
2. You have pending requests that use up your balance
3. HR hasn't initialized credit for this leave type yet
4. You're requesting a leave type not configured for your organization

**How to Fix**:
1. **Check your balance**: Go to **Leaves** → **Balance** to see exact numbers
2. **Wait for pending approval**: If you have pending requests, wait for manager to approve/reject
3. **Request different leave type**: If you have no vacation days, try sick leave or personal leave
4. **Contact HR**: If you believe your allocation is wrong, HR can adjust credits

**Prevention**:
- Check available balance BEFORE requesting leave
- Request leave with enough buffer (don't use 100% of annual allocation at once)
- Plan leave strategically throughout the year

#### ❌ Error: "Insufficient balance after deduction"

**What This Means**: After all pending approvals, you don't have enough credit for this new request.

**Example**:
```
You have: 20 vacation days
Pending request: 10 days
Available: 10 days

You try to request: 12 days
System says: Can't approve—only 10 days available
```

**How to Fix**:
1. Reduce the number of days in your request
2. Cancel or modify pending requests to free up credit
3. Request a smaller leave period first
4. Contact manager to approve pending requests faster

### How HR Initializes Credits

**HR Administrators create leave credits** before employees can request leave. This typically happens:

1. **At Hire Date**: HR creates initial allocations
2. **Annually**: HR resets or increases allocations each year
3. **Per Quarter**: Some organizations allocate quarterly

**Important**: No employees can request leave until HR initializes credit for that leave type.

**If you can't request leave**:
- Your organization may be in setup phase
- HR hasn't initialized credit yet
- You may not be configured as an active employee
- Contact your HR team

### Leave Types and Credit Independence

Each leave type is independent:

| Leave Type | Credit Source | When Used | Balance |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|---------|
| **Vacation** | Annual allocation | Flexible | Tracked separately |
| **Sick Leave** | Annual allocation | As needed | Tracked separately |
| **Personal** | Annual allocation | Personal matters | Tracked separately |
| **Bereavement** | May be unlimited | Family death | Per company policy |
| **Unpaid Leave** | No credit needed | Can use anytime | N/A |

**Key Point**: Using 5 days of vacation doesn't affect your sick leave credit.

### Carryover and Expiration

**Carryover** (if your company allows):
- Unused leave may roll over to next year
- Usually with a limit (e.g., max 5 carryover days)
- May expire on a specific date

**Example**:
```
2025: You had 20 days, used 15, had 5 remaining
2026:
├── New allocation: 20 days
├── Carryover: 5 days (expires Dec 31, 2026)
└── Total for 2026: 25 days
```

**Expiration**:
- Carryover days typically expire at year-end
- Once expired, you lose that balance
- Use carryover early in the year if approaching expiration date

### What Happens After Manager Approval

Once your request is **approved**:

1. **Credits deducted**: Balance decreases permanently
2. **Appears on timesheet**: Your timesheet shows approved leave
3. **Marked in calendar**: Leave is visible on leave calendar
4. **Payroll includes it**: Payslip shows paid leave

Once **rejected**:
1. **Credits released**: Your balance returns to normal
2. **Reason provided**: Manager gives reason for rejection
3. **Can re-request**: After fixing the issue, you can request again

## Leave Types

Kando supports multiple leave types. Each organization may have different leave policies:

### Paid Leave Types

**Vacation Leave (Annual Leave)**
- Used for personal holidays and vacations
- Typically allocated annually
- May carry over based on company policy
- Example: Approved for 2 weeks of vacation

**Sick Leave**
- Used when you're ill or have medical appointments
- May require medical documentation for extended periods
- Usually doesn't require advance notice
- Example: Approved for 3 days due to illness

**Personal Leave**
- Used for personal matters (appointments, emergencies)
- Limited number of days per year
- May require manager approval
- Example: Approved for 1 day for family obligations

**Bereavement Leave**
- Used when a family member passes away
- May have specific approval process
- Usually paid
- Example: Approved for 5 days

### Unpaid Leave

**Unpaid Leave**
- Taken without pay
- May require special approval
- Appears on timesheet as unpaid
- Example: Extended leave without income

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Check Your Leave Balance

1. Go to **Dashboard** on your home page
2. Look for the **Leave Balance** widget
3. You'll see:
   - Total leave available for the year
   - Leave already taken
   - Leave pending approval
   - Leave remaining

Example:
```
Vacation Leave: 20 days
├── Used: 5 days
├── Pending: 3 days
└── Available: 12 days
```

### Step 2: Request Leave

1. Click the **Leave** button in the Dashboard Quick Actions
2. Or navigate to **Leaves** → **Request Leave**
3. Fill in the leave request form:
   - **Leave Type**: Select from dropdown (Vacation, Sick, Personal, etc.)
   - **Start Date**: Click calendar to select first day
   - **End Date**: Click calendar to select last day
   - **Reason**: Explain why you need leave (especially important for managers)
   - **Attachments**: Add medical certificate if required (for sick leave)
   - **Manager Notes**: Add any additional information
4. Click **Submit Request**

**📱 Also on mobile**: Submit leave requests, attach documents, and check your balance history from the Kando mobile app — see [Kando Mobile App](mobile-app.md).

### Step 3: Receive Approval

1. Your manager will review your request
2. You'll receive a notification when approved or rejected
3. Approved leave appears on:
   - Your dashboard (updated balance)
   - Your timesheet
   - Your calendar

## Common Tasks

### Request Vacation Leave

**Scenario**: You want to take 2 weeks off for a summer vacation

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to **Leaves** → **New Request**
2. **Leave Type**: Select "Vacation Leave"
3. **Start Date**: July 15, 2026
4. **End Date**: July 26, 2026 (10 business days)
5. **Reason**: "Summer vacation with family"
6. **Check Impact**: See how many days remaining after approval
7. Click **Submit**
8. Wait for manager approval (usually 1-2 business days)

**Keyboard Shortcut**: `Ctrl+L` opens leave request form

### Request Sick Leave

**Scenario**: You're unexpectedly ill and need to take sick leave today

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to **Leaves** → **New Request**
2. **Leave Type**: Select "Sick Leave"
3. **Start Date**: Today (date auto-fills)
4. **End Date**: Today
5. **Reason**: "Not feeling well, doctor appointment scheduled"
6. **Attachment**: Upload doctor's note if available (optional for 1 day)
7. Click **Submit**
8. Typically auto-approved for 1-2 days without documentation

**Important**: Report sick leave to your manager via phone/message, then request in Kando within 24 hours

### Request Partial Day Leave

**Scenario**: You need to leave at 12:00 PM for a medical appointment

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to **Leaves** → **New Request**
2. **Leave Type**: Select appropriate type (Sick Leave, Personal Leave)
3. **Date**: Select the date
4. **Duration**: Select "Half Day" or "Quarter Day"
5. **Time**: Specify when you'll leave (e.g., "Leave at 12:00 PM")
6. **Reason**: "Medical appointment at 2:00 PM"
7. Click **Submit**

**Note**: Partial day leaves are calculated based on your normal working hours

### Check Leave Status

**Find Pending Requests**:
1. Navigate to **Leaves** → **My Requests**
2. View all requests with status:
   - 🟢 **Approved**: Already approved, scheduled
   - 🟡 **Pending**: Awaiting manager approval
   - 🔴 **Rejected**: Declined by manager
   - ⚪ **Cancelled**: Previously cancelled

**Find Past Leave**:
1. Click **View History** tab
2. Filter by:
   - **Date Range**: Select custom dates
   - **Leave Type**: Filter by type
   - **Status**: Approved, Rejected, Cancelled

### Update or Cancel Leave Request

**Before Approval (Pending)**:
1. Go to **Leaves** → **My Requests**
2. Find the pending request
3. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon)
4. Change dates, reason, or attachments
5. Click **Save Changes**

**Scenario**: You accidentally selected the wrong dates
- Before manager reviews, edit the request immediately

**After Approval**:
1. Go to **Leaves** → **My Requests**
2. Find the approved request
3. Click **Cancel Request**
4. Provide reason: "Plans changed" or similar
5. Manager may need to re-approve timesheet

**Important**: Cancelling approved leave may affect payroll, notify your manager

## Tips & Tricks

✅ **DO**:
- Request vacation leave **2-4 weeks in advance** for better approval chances
- Include a clear reason for leave requests, especially personal leave
- Check your leave balance **before requesting** to avoid exceeding limits
- **Notify your team** informally before submitting formal leave request
- Attach medical certificates for sick leave lasting more than 2 days
- **Save drafts** of complex leave requests (partially fill and save)
- Check your **manager's calendar** to avoid peak busy periods
- **Review company policies** in onboarding documents for leave regulations
- Mark urgent projects as **"out of office"** when taking extended leave
- **Escalate approvals** to skip-level manager if direct manager is on leave

❌ **DON'T**:
- Request excessive leave in short periods without clear reason
- Ignore leave policy requirements or documentation needs
- Request leave while on probation (check company policy)
- Assume approval—wait for official notification
- Submit multiple overlapping leave requests
- Cancel approved leave without notifying your manager
- Use sick leave without actual illness (policy violation)
- Request leave retroactively (after the date passes)
- Exceed your leave balance in a single request
- Forget to update your manager if plans change after requesting leave

## Leave Approval Process

### How Managers Approve Leave

1. Manager receives notification of pending request
2. Manager reviews:
   - Your available balance
   - Team coverage and workload
   - Leave policy compliance
3. Manager approves or rejects
4. You receive notification

### Automatic Rejection Scenarios

Your request may be automatically rejected if:
- Insufficient leave balance
- Leave dates are company holidays
- Outside allowed leave period (policy-dependent)
- Duplicate request for same dates

**Action**: Contact your manager or HR to discuss alternatives

### What If Leave Is Rejected?

1. You'll receive rejection notification with reason
2. Possible reasons:
   - Insufficient balance
   - Manager has conflicting priority
   - Policy violation
   - Team coverage issue
3. Options:
   - Discuss with manager to find alternative dates
   - Submit updated request with different dates
   - Request unpaid leave as alternative
   - Escalate to skip-level manager if needed

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much vacation leave do I get per year?**
A: Annual leave allocation depends on your role, seniority, and company policy. Check your **Leave Balance** in the Dashboard or contact HR. Typical allocation is 15-20 working days.

**Q: Can I split my vacation leave?**
A: Yes, you can request multiple shorter leave periods throughout the year. Most companies allow this flexibly. Contact your HR team for specific policy details.

**Q: What happens if I don't use all my leave?**
A: Some organizations allow carryover of unused leave to the next year (typically with a limit like 5-10 days). Others require you to take leave annually. Check your company policy or contact HR.

**Q: Can I request leave if I'm on probation?**
A: Most companies don't allow leave during probation period (first 3-6 months). Check your employment agreement or contact HR for specific policy.

**Q: Do I get paid during leave?**
A: Yes, approved paid leave (vacation, sick leave, personal leave) is paid by your company. Unpaid leave is not compensated. Your payslip will show leave details.

**Q: What if my manager is on leave when I need approval?**
A: You can request approval from a skip-level manager or delegated manager during your direct manager's absence. Contact HR to set up delegation.

**Q: Can I request leave retroactively?**
A: Retroactive leave requests are generally not allowed. You must request leave before or on the day it's taken (with manager pre-approval or within 24 hours for emergencies).

**Q: How is partial day leave calculated?**
A: Partial day leave (half-day or quarter-day) is deducted proportionally from your annual leave. Example: Half-day = 0.5 days deducted, Quarter-day = 0.25 days deducted.

**Q: What if I'm sick but can work from home?**
A: If you're healthy enough to work, you don't need to take sick leave. Work from home as normal. Sick leave is for when you're unable to work due to illness.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: "Request was auto-rejected – Insufficient Leave Balance"
**Cause**: You've requested more days than available
**Solution**:
1. Check your current balance in Dashboard
2. Request fewer days within your available balance
3. Ask manager about advance leave or unpaid leave options
4. Contact HR if there's a discrepancy in your balance

**Problem**: "I can't see the Leave module in Kando"
**Cause**: Leave feature may not be enabled for your organization or role
**Solution**:
1. Check if Leave is available in your main navigation
2. Contact HR to verify leave is enabled
3. Your organization may use different leave management system
4. Escalate to your manager if uncertain

**Problem**: "My leave request shows 'Pending' for more than 5 days"
**Cause**: Manager delayed in reviewing, or request is waiting for escalation
**Solution**:
1. Send informal reminder to your manager
2. Check if manager is out of office (delegate approval)
3. Contact HR if approval is significantly delayed (SLA: 2-3 business days)
4. Check if additional documentation is required (attachments)

**Problem**: "The calendar shows wrong dates for my approved leave"
**Cause**: System timezone difference or date range calculation issue
**Solution**:
1. Check your timezone in **Settings** → **Preferences**
2. Verify leave request shows correct dates
3. Refresh page (Ctrl+R) to reload calendar
4. Contact support if issue persists

**Problem**: "I requested leave but system shows different number of days"
**Cause**: Partial days, weekends, or company holidays not included in count
**Solution**:
1. Leave calculation excludes weekends and company holidays automatically
2. If you requested Mon-Fri, that's 5 calendar days but may count as different working days
3. Contact HR to clarify leave calculation policy
4. Review leave balance details for detailed breakdown

**Problem**: "Can't edit my request – button is greyed out"
**Cause**: Request is already approved or in manager's final review stage
**Solution**:
1. If request is approved, you need to **Cancel** instead of edit
2. If pending, wait a moment and refresh page
3. Contact HR if you need to change an approved leave request

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Emergency Leave
**Situation**: Your child is ill and you need to leave work immediately

**Actions**:
1. Call your manager immediately (before Kando)
2. Notify your team leads
3. Log into Kando from home/car
4. Navigate to **Leaves** → **New Request**
5. Request today as Sick Leave with reason "Family emergency"
6. Attach any documentation if required
7. Follow up with manager to confirm understanding

**Timeframe**: Submit within 24 hours for immediate leave

### Scenario 2: Planned Long-Term Leave
**Situation**: You're planning a 3-week overseas trip

**Actions**:
1. Check your annual leave balance (should have ~15+ days available)
2. Discuss informally with your manager 1 month before
3. In Kando, submit formal request 2-3 weeks before
4. Select all dates and provide clear purpose: "Overseas vacation"
5. Mention team handover plan if needed
6. Follow up after 1 week if not approved
7. Once approved, inform your team and handover work

**Approval Rate**: High (95%+) when requested in advance with clear dates

### Scenario 3: Recurring Leave Pattern
**Situation**: You have a weekly medical appointment every Tuesday

**Actions**:
1. Discuss with manager if recurring pattern is possible
2. Request first appointment leave through Kando
3. Once approved, submit additional weeks as separate requests
4. Provide medical documentation for recurring pattern
5. Consider discussing flexible work arrangement instead
6. If approved as pattern, leave appears automatically

**Alternative**: Request compressed work hours or flexible timing instead
