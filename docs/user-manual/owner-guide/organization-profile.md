# Organization Profile

*Set up and manage your organization's basic information, logo, and billing contact details.*

## Overview

Your organization profile contains the core information that identifies your company in Kando. This includes your organization name, logo, subdomain, and billing contact information. Only owners can edit this information.

## Accessing Organization Profile

1. Click **Settings** in the main menu
2. Select **Organization** from the sidebar
3. Click the **Profile** tab
4. You'll see your current organization information

## Editing Organization Profile

### Step 1: Click Edit Profile

1. Navigate to Settings > Organization > Profile
2. Click the **Edit Profile** button
3. The profile form will open

### Step 2: Update Profile Information

Update any of these fields:

- **Organization Name** (Required)
  - Your company's official name
  - Used in documents and reports
  - Must be 3-255 characters

- **Address** (Optional)
  - Physical or mailing address
  - Used for billing and invoices
  - Can include street, city, state, postal code

- **Subdomain** (Required)
  - Your unique organization subdomain
  - Used in your Kando URL (https://subdomain.kando.com)
  - Must be unique (cannot change after creation)
  - Lowercase letters and hyphens only

- **Billing Name** (Required)
  - Person responsible for billing
  - Should be someone who approves payments
  - Used in all billing communications

- **Billing Email** (Required)
  - Email for all billing notifications and invoices
  - Verify this email after updating
  - Invoices and renewal notices sent here

- **Billing Phone** (Optional)
  - Contact number for billing inquiries
  - Should have someone available to answer
  - Format: Include country code if international

### Step 3: Upload Organization Logo

Your organization logo appears in:
- Kando sidebar (top-left)
- Employee timesheets and reports
- Email templates
- Mobile app

**To upload logo:**

1. Click the **logo upload area** in the edit form
2. Select an image file from your computer
   - **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WEBP
   - **Size**: Maximum 5 MB
   - **Recommended dimensions**: 300x300 pixels (square) or 600x200 pixels (wide)
3. The logo preview will appear
4. Click **Save Changes**

**Logo Tips:**
✅ Use a high-quality image for best appearance
✅ Choose a logo that's recognizable at small sizes
✅ Use transparent background for PNG files
✅ Test logo in dark/light theme for visibility
❌ Don't use overly large files (>5 MB)
❌ Don't use low-resolution logos (< 100x100 pixels)

### Step 4: Save Changes

1. Review all your changes
2. Click **Save Changes** button
3. Your profile will be updated
4. A confirmation message will appear

## Common Scenarios

### I need to update our company name

1. Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
2. Click **Edit Profile**
3. Update the **Organization Name** field
4. Click **Save Changes**
5. The new name appears immediately in the system

### We changed our billing contact person

1. Open the organization profile
2. Click **Edit Profile**
3. Update **Billing Name** and **Billing Email**
4. Verify the new email address (confirmation email will be sent)
5. Click **Save Changes**
6. Future billing notifications will go to the new contact

### I want to update our organization logo

1. Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
2. Click **Edit Profile**
3. Click the **logo upload area**
4. Select your new logo image
5. Review the preview
6. Click **Save Changes**
7. Logo updates appear system-wide within seconds

### Billing email is not receiving notifications

1. Check that the billing email was saved correctly
2. Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
3. Click **Edit Profile** and verify **Billing Email**
4. Look for verification email from Kando
5. If no verification email received, request email verification
6. Check spam/junk folder
7. Update email if it was incorrect, then save

## Verification and Confirmation

### Email Verification

When you update the billing email:

1. A verification email is sent to the new address
2. The new billing email is marked as "unverified"
3. Click the verification link in the email within 24 hours
4. Email is confirmed and active for billing communications

**If verification link expires:**
- Request a new verification email from the profile settings
- Complete verification before continuing
- Unverified emails cannot receive important billing notifications

### Changes Take Effect Immediately

- Organization name updates appear immediately system-wide
- Logo updates appear within 2-3 seconds
- Billing contact changes take effect for next communication
- Address changes update on next invoice generation
