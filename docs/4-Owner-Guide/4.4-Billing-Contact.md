# Billing Contact

*Manage billing information and ensure important payment notifications reach the right person.*

## Overview

Your billing contact is the person responsible for receiving and approving billing-related communications from Kando. This includes invoices, renewal notices, payment receipts, and subscription updates. Keeping this information current is essential for smooth subscription management.

## Understanding Billing Contacts

### What is a Billing Contact?

A **billing contact** is the designated person in your organization who:
- Receives invoices and payment reminders
- Approves renewal requests
- Makes payment decisions
- Updates billing information
- Manages organization's Kando subscription budget

**Typically:**
- Finance department representative
- CFO or Finance Manager
- Operations Manager
- Company Owner/Director

## Accessing Billing Information

1. Click **Settings** in the main menu
2. Select **Organization** from the sidebar
3. Click the **Profile** tab
4. Look for the **Billing Contact** section

### Current Billing Contact Information

You'll see the current contact details:

| Field | Information | Used For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Name** | Billing contact person's name | Email salutations, ID purposes |
| **Email** | Primary billing communication address | Invoices, renewal notices, receipts |
| **Phone** | Contact phone number | Payment issues, urgent matters |

## Updating Billing Contact

### Prerequisites

- You must be the organization owner
- The new contact must exist in the organization

### Step 1: Go to Edit Profile

1. Navigate to Settings > Organization > Profile
2. Click the **Edit Profile** button
3. The edit form opens

### Step 2: Update Billing Information

You can update these fields:

**Billing Name** (Required)
- Full name of the billing contact
- Example: "John Smith" or "Finance Manager"
- This is who receives the communications
- Should be someone available to approve payments

**Billing Email** (Required)
- Email address for all billing communications
- Should be monitored regularly
- Will receive:
  - Monthly invoices
  - Renewal reminders (30, 7, and 1 day before expiry)
  - Payment confirmations
  - Subscription updates
  - Special promotions and announcements

**Billing Phone** (Optional but Recommended)
- Phone number for billing inquiries
- Used if urgent payment issues arise
- Format with country code if international
- Examples: "+1-555-0123" or "+63-917-123-4567"

### Step 3: Save Changes

1. Review your updates
2. Click **Save Changes**
3. Confirmation message appears
4. Changes take effect immediately

### Email Verification

**Important:** When updating the billing email:

1. A verification email is sent to the new address
2. The new email is marked as "pending verification"
3. The billing contact must click the verification link
4. Email is confirmed when link is clicked
5. If not verified within 24 hours, a new link is sent

**If verification fails:**
- Check spam/junk folder
- Request new verification email
- Verify the email address was entered correctly
- Ensure email account is active

## Billing Communications

### What Your Billing Contact Receives

**Monthly Communications:**

| Communication | Frequency | Content |
|------------------|-----------|---------|
| **Invoice** | Monthly | Detailed billing for that month |
| **Renewal Reminder** | 30 days before expiry | "Your subscription expires in 30 days" |
| **Urgent Reminder** | 7 days before expiry | "Your subscription expires in 7 days" |
| **Final Notice** | 1 day before expiry | "Your subscription expires tomorrow" |
| **Payment Confirmation** | Upon payment | Receipt and acknowledgment |
| **Invoice Duplicate** | Upon request | Copy of previous invoice |

## Common Scenarios

### Changing from HR Manager to Finance Manager for billing

1. Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
2. Click **Edit Profile**
3. Update **Billing Name** to Finance Manager's name
4. Update **Billing Email** to Finance Manager's email
5. Ensure Finance Manager clicks verification link
6. Click **Save Changes**
7. Finance Manager will receive all future billing emails

### Our billing email is not receiving invoices

**Troubleshooting steps:**

1. Check spam/junk folder for Kando emails
2. Add Kando billing email to safe senders
3. Verify current billing email in Settings > Organization > Profile
4. If email is incorrect, update it and verify new email
5. Check with email provider if mail server is blocking emails
6. Contact support if issue persists

**To verify current billing email:**
1. Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
2. Look at Billing Email field
3. If showing "Unverified", request new verification email
4. Click verification link within 24 hours

### New finance person is joining, need to update

1. Get new person's contact details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
2. Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
3. Click **Edit Profile**
4. Update Billing Name to new person's name
5. Update Billing Email to new email
6. Update Billing Phone if applicable
7. New person verifies their email
8. Click **Save Changes**

### We need a shared inbox for billing emails

1. If possible, set up a shared email address for billing
2. Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
3. Click **Edit Profile**
4. Set Billing Email to shared inbox (e.g., `billing@acme.com`)
5. Ensure shared inbox is monitored
6. Verify the shared email address
7. Click **Save Changes**
8. All billing communications now go to shared inbox

### Need to add backup billing contact

**Option 1: Update to backup person (when primary is unavailable)**
1. Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
2. Click **Edit Profile**
3. Change Billing Email to backup person's email
4. Save Changes
5. Switch back to primary person's email when they return

**Option 2: Contact Support**
- Request to add CC or BCC to billing emails
- Support can help set up additional recipients
- Available for enterprise customers

### I'm leaving the company, need to transfer to successor

**Before you leave:**

1. Brief your successor on:
   - How to manage subscriptions
   - When renewal dates occur
   - Monthly billing process
   - Payment approval process

2. Update billing contact:
   - Go to Settings > Organization > Profile
   - Click **Edit Profile**
   - Update to successor's name and email
   - Ask successor to verify email
   - Save Changes

3. Share important documents:
   - Contract terms
   - Payment history
   - Subscription list
   - Renewal calendar

## Best Practices

✅ **DO**:
- Keep billing email current (update when person changes)
- Verify billing email to ensure delivery
- Monitor for renewal reminders
- Plan for subscription renewals in advance
- Respond to billing communications promptly
- Update phone number if it's a direct line

❌ **DON'T**:
- Use a personal/vacation email for billing
- Leave billing email unverified
- Ignore renewal reminders (access may be interrupted)
- Delay updating when billing contact leaves
- Use email without checking for mail delivery issues
- Assume emails will arrive (check spam folder)
