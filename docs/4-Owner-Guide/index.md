# Owner Guide

Welcome to the **Kando Owner Guide** - your complete resource for managing your organization's subscription, billing, and organizational settings.

## 👨‍💼 Who is an Owner?

As an **Organization Owner**, you are responsible for:
- Managing your organization's billing and subscription
- Setting up and maintaining organization details
- Configuring organization-wide settings
- Managing subscription seats and user licenses
- Overseeing billing and auto-renewal settings

## 📚 Owner Guide Sections

### **4.1 - Organization Profile**
Manage your organization's basic information, logo, and billing contact details.
- Organization name and subdomain
- Upload and manage organization logo
- Billing contact information (name, email, phone)
- Address information

### **4.2 - Organization Settings**
Configure organization-wide settings that affect all users.
- Timezone (affects all time displays)
- Date and time format preferences
- Employee name display format
- Department management
- Week start day configuration

### **4.3 - Subscription Management**
Manage your subscription seats and user licenses.
- View all subscription seats
- Assign and unassign users to seats
- Renew expired seats
- Enable/disable auto-renewal
- Filter and search subscriptions
- View seat statistics

### **4.4 - Billing Contact**
Update billing information and manage subscription payments.
- Billing name, email, and phone
- Organization address for billing
- Payment method information
- Billing history and invoices
- Subscription renewal settings

## 🔑 Key Responsibilities

- **First 24 Hours**: Set up organization profile with your company details
- **First Week**: Configure organization settings (timezone, date formats, departments)
- **First Month**: Assign subscription seats to your team members
- **Ongoing**: Monitor subscription usage and manage auto-renewal

## ✅ Best Practices

✅ **DO**:
- Set timezone correctly to match your organization's location
- Assign auto-renewal for critical employee seats
- Keep billing contact information up-to-date
- Regularly review subscription usage
- Document your organization structure

❌ **DON'T**:
- Assume default settings will work for your organization
- Leave billing contact email unverified
- Delete seats without unassigning users first
- Ignore renewal reminders
- Keep outdated organization information
