# Dashboard Overview

Your **Dashboard** is your home page in Kando. It shows your key information and quick access to common tasks.

## What You See on Your Dashboard

### Top Section: Quick Actions
Large buttons for your most common tasks:
- **Clock In/Out** - Start or end your work day
- **Request Leave** - Request time off
- **View Schedule** - See your upcoming shifts
- **Submit Timesheet** - Submit your weekly work hours

Click any button to perform that action immediately.

### Main Area: Your Status
Information about your current activity:
- **Current Status** - Are you clocked in or out?
- **Time Worked Today** - Hours logged so far
- **Last Clocked In** - When you clocked in
- **Next Shift** - Your next scheduled shift

### Left Menu: Navigation
Access all features from the menu:
- **Dashboard** - Return to home (current page)
- **Time** - Time tracking and timesheets
- **Leave** - Leave requests and balance
- **Schedule** - Your schedule and shifts
- **Pay** - Payroll and payslips
- **Settings** - Your preferences

### Main Content: Your Information

**Today's Information**:
- Your clocking status
- Breaks taken
- Time logged
- Pending approvals (if manager)

**Upcoming**:
- Your next shift
- Upcoming leave
- Pending requests
- Important notifications

**Recent Activity**:
- Last 5 clock ins/outs
- Recent timesheet submissions
- Leave request history

## Common Dashboard Tasks

### Check Your Status
**Goal**: Is your time being tracked?

**Steps**:
1. Look at the **Current Status** section
2. If it says "Clocked In" - You're tracking time ✅
3. If it says "Clocked Out" - You're not tracking time
4. To clock in, click **Clock In** button

### View Your Time Today
**Goal**: How many hours have you worked today?

**Steps**:
1. Look at **Time Worked Today** in the status area
2. You'll see total hours logged
3. Click **Time Tracking** for detailed breakdown

### Find Your Next Shift
**Goal**: When do you work next?

**Steps**:
1. Look at **Upcoming Shifts**
2. See the date and time of your next shift
3. Click **View Schedule** for full calendar view

### Check Your Leave Balance
**Goal**: How much leave do you have left?

**Steps**:
1. Look for **Leave Balance** section
2. See available leave hours/days
3. Click **Request Leave** to request time off

### Review Your Pay
**Goal**: View your latest paycheck

**Steps**:
1. Look at **Latest Payslip** (if available)
2. Click **View Payslips** for full history
3. Download or view detailed breakdown

## Customizing Your Dashboard

### Change Dashboard Theme
1. Click **Settings** (gear icon)
2. Choose **Theme** - Light or Dark mode
3. Save your preference

### Change Your Avatar
1. Click **Profile** (your name/avatar)
2. Click **Change Avatar**
3. Upload a photo or choose default
4. Save

### Update Your Information
1. Click **Settings**
2. Click **Personal Information**
3. Update your details
4. Click **Save**

**Pending Items**:
- Leave requests to approve

**Team Metrics**:

### Manager Quick Actions
Additional buttons for managers:
- **Create Schedule** - Plan next week's shifts

## Tips & Tricks

### Bookmark Important Pages
Use browser bookmarks for:
- Dashboard (home)
- Your schedule
- Leave management
- Payslips

**How**: Right-click page → Bookmark

### Use Search
Can't find something? Use the search bar:
- Search for features
- Search for help topics
- Search for reports

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Ctrl+H** - Go to Dashboard (Home)
- **Ctrl+T** - Go to Time tracking
- **Ctrl+L** - Go to Leave management
- **Ctrl+Q** - Quick Clock In/Out

See [Keyboard Shortcuts](../7-Reference/7.2-Keyboard-Shortcuts.md) for more.

### Notifications
**Bell Icon** (top-right):
- Shows alerts and updates
- Click to see full list
- Some are "urgent" (red badge)

**Set Preferences**:
1. Click **Settings**
2. Choose **Notifications**
3. Select what you want to be notified about

## Troubleshooting Dashboard

### Dashboard Won't Load
- Refresh page (F5)
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser
- Check internet connection

### Information Looks Wrong
- Refresh page
- Logout and login again
- Check with your manager or HR
- Contact support

### I Don't See a Feature
- It might be disabled for your role
- You might not have permission
- Check with your HR department
- Upgrade to latest browser

### Buttons Don't Work
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser
- Contact support if still broken
