# Introduction to Kando

Welcome to **Kando** - your complete workforce management system. This page introduces you to Kando and what you can do with it.

## What is Kando?

Kando is a software system that helps organizations manage their workforce efficiently. It handles:

- **Time Tracking** - Clock in/out, track work hours
- **Leave Management** - Request and track time off
- **Scheduling** - Create and manage work schedules
- **Payroll** - Calculate and process employee pay
- **Approval Workflows** - Route requests for approval
- **Reporting** - View data and generate reports

## Who Uses Kando?

### Employees
- Track your work hours
- Request leave and time off
- View your schedule
- Check your payslip
- Submit timesheets

### Managers
- View your team's timesheets
- Approve leave and time requests
- Create and manage schedules
- Generate team reports
- Monitor attendance

### HR Administrators
- Configure the system
- Manage users and access
- Set leave and work policies
- Process payroll
- Generate compliance reports

## Key Features

### 🕐 Time Tracking
- **Clock In/Out**: Record when you start and end work
- **Manual Entries**: Add time entries if you forget to clock in
- **Timesheet Review**: Submit timesheets for approval
- **Time Tracking**: Track daily work hours

### 📅 Leave Management
- **Request Leave**: Submit leave requests
- **Leave Balance**: Check how much leave you have available
- **Approval Workflow**: Managers review and approve requests
- **History**: View past leave requests

### 📊 Scheduling
- **View Schedules**: See your assigned shifts
- **Shift Swap**: Request to swap shifts with colleagues
- **Team Schedules**: View team member schedules
- **Schedule Planning**: Create and adjust schedules

### 💰 Payroll
- **Payslip Viewing**: See detailed pay information
- **Pay History**: View past paychecks
- **Deductions**: Understand salary deductions
- **Tax Information**: Access tax documents

## How to Navigate Kando

### Main Menu
The main menu (usually at the top or left) lets you access different areas:
- **Dashboard** - Your home page with key information
- **Time** - Time tracking and timesheet features
- **Leave** - Leave request and balance features
- **Schedule** - Schedule viewing and management
- **Pay** - Payroll and compensation features
- **Settings** - Your personal settings and preferences

### Dashboard
Your personal dashboard shows:
- Quick links to common tasks
- Recent activity
- Important notifications
- Pending approvals (if you're a manager)
- Your team status (if you're a manager)

## Getting Started Steps

### Step 1: Login
Visit your Kando link and enter your:
- **Username** - Your employee ID or email
- **Password** - Your system password

### Step 2: Complete Your Profile
Update your personal information:
- Contact details
- Department
- Manager information
- Bank details (for payroll)

### Step 3: Explore the Dashboard
Spend a few minutes getting familiar with:
- Menu options
- Available features
- Where to find common tasks

### Step 4: Perform Your First Task
Try a simple task like:
- Clock in/out
- View your schedule
- Check leave balance

### Step 5: Review Your First Document
Once data starts flowing, review:
- Your timesheet
- Your payslip
- Leave request status

## Important Concepts

### Organization
Your employer or company within Kando.

### Department
Your team or division within the organization.

### Employee
An individual person in the system (you).

### Manager
Your direct supervisor who approves your requests.

### Leave Balance
The amount of leave you have available to use.

### Timesheet
A summary of your work hours for a period (usually weekly).

### Shift
A scheduled work period (e.g., 9 AM - 5 PM).

### Payslip
Your payment document showing salary, deductions, and net pay.

### Approval Workflow
The process of routing requests (leave, overtime) to managers for approval.

## Troubleshooting First Steps

### "I forgot my password"
- Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
- Follow the email instructions

### "I can't login"
- Check your internet connection
- Verify your username and password
- Try a different browser
- Contact IT support if still stuck

### "I don't see a feature"
- You might not have permission (check with your manager)
- It might be disabled for your organization
- Refresh the page to reload
- Clear your browser cache

### "Something looks wrong"
- Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache
- Contact support if problem persists
