# Employee Onboarding Strategy

## 🎯 Goal
Get the employee to **Clock In** and view their **Schedule** within the first 10 minutes of login.

## 👤 User Mindset
- "I just want to get paid."
- "I don't want to break anything."
- "Where do I see my shifts?"
- "How do I ask for time off?"

## 🗺️ The "Happy Path"
1. **Welcome Modal**: Brief greeting + "Let's get you set up" button.
2. **Profile Confirmation**: Quick verify of name/email (pre-filled by HR).
3. **Choice**: "Take the Tour" or "Explore Myself".
4. **Interactive Tour**:
   - Highlight **Clock In** button.
   - Highlight **Schedule** widget.
   - Highlight **Leave Balance**.
5. **Checklist**: A persistent "Getting Started" widget on the dashboard until 100% complete.

## ✅ Onboarding Milestones (Checklist)
This checklist appears on the Employee Dashboard:
1. **Verify Profile**: Confirm contact details.
2. **First Clock-In**: Successfully clock in for a shift.
3. **Check Schedule**: View the "My Schedule" page.
4. **View Leave Balance**: Navigate to the Leave page.
5. **Notification Setup**: Choose email/SMS preferences.

## 💡 Convenience Features
- **Auto-Suggest Clock In**: If the user logs in during their scheduled shift start time, show a prominent "Clock In Now?" prompt.
- **Empty State Zero-Data**: If they have no schedule yet, show a friendly "No shifts assigned yet" message rather than a broken empty table.
- **Mobile First**: Ensure all these onboarding steps work seamlessly on mobile, as many employees will clock in via phone.
