# Welcome, Manager! 👨‍💼

As a manager in Kando, you are the captain of your team's ship. You'll use Kando to approve requests, build schedules, and ensure your team is happy and productive.

This guide will help you master the basics in about **20 minutes**.

---

## ✅ Your Manager Checklist

### 1. [ ] complete Your Profile
**Goal**: Set a good example.
- Update your contact info and photo in **Settings** → **My Profile**.
- *Time: 2 mins*

### 2. [ ] Review Your Team Roster
**Goal**: Ensure everyone you manage is actually on your list.
- Go to the **Team** tab.
- Browse the list of employees.
- If anyone is missing or you see someone who shouldn't be there, contact HR immediately.
- *Time: 5 mins*

### 3. [ ] Set Approval Notifications
**Goal**: Never miss a leave request.
- Go to **Settings** → **Notifications**.
- Ensure **"New Leave Request"** and **"TS Submission"** are set to **Instant** (Email or Push).
- Fast approvals = Happy team!
- *Time: 2 mins*

### 4. [ ] Review a Sample Request
**Goal**: Practice the approval workflow.
- Go to **Approvals** (check mark icon).
- You might see a sample/training request or a real one.
- Click to view details.
- Practice clicking **Approve** or **Reject** (you can undo this later if it's a test).
- *Time: 3 mins*

### 5. [ ] Create Your First Schedule
**Goal**: Plan the week ahead.
- Go to **Schedule** → **Team Schedule**.
- Try dragging a shift onto a team member's calendar row.
- Click **Publish** to make it visible (or "Save Draft" if you're just testing).
- *Time: 5 mins*

### 6. [ ] Generate a Report
**Goal**: See data insights.
- Go to **Reports**.
- Run a "Team Attendance" report for the last 30 days.
- See who is consistently on time!
- *Time: 3 mins*

---

## 💡 Leadership Tips

- **Approve Fast**: Try to approve leave requests within 24 hours. The system will remind you if you forget!
- **Monitor Overtime**: Keep an eye on the "Overtime Watch" widget on your dashboard to prevent burnout (and budget overruns).
- **Delegation**: Going on vacation yourself? Use "Delegation Settings" to assign a temporary approver while you're away.
