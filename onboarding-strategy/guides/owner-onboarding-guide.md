# Welcome, Organization Owner! 💳

As the Organization Owner, you are responsible for managing your company's subscription, billing, and organizational settings in Kando. You'll set up your organization, manage subscription seats, and ensure your team has the access they need.

This guide will help you get everything configured in about **15 minutes**.

---

## ✅ Your Owner Checklist

### 1. [ ] Complete Your Organization Profile
**Goal**: Make your organization official.
- Go to **Settings** → **Organization** → **Profile**.
- Upload your company logo.
- Enter organization name and address.
- Fill in billing contact information (name, email, phone).
- *Time: 5 mins*

### 2. [ ] Configure Organization Settings
**Goal**: Set up preferences for your entire organization.
- Go to **Settings** → **Organization** → **Settings**.
- Select timezone (where your payroll is processed).
- Choose date and time format.
- Set employee name display format.
- *Time: 3 mins*

### 3. [ ] Verify Billing Contact Email
**Goal**: Ensure invoices reach the right person.
- Check your inbox for verification email from Kando.
- Click the verification link.
- Confirm billing contact is set correctly.
- *Time: 2 mins*

### 4. [ ] Review Your Subscription Seats
**Goal**: Know what you've purchased and usage.
- Go to **Settings** → **Organization** → **Subscriptions**.
- View total seats vs. active seats.
- Check subscription expiry dates.
- Note any seats about to expire.
- *Time: 3 mins*

### 5. [ ] Assign Seats to Key Staff
**Goal**: Get your team productive immediately.
- Find unassigned seats in subscriptions table.
- Assign at least one seat to your HR Admin or Manager.
- Enable auto-renewal for critical seats.
- *Time: 2 mins*

---

## 💡 Ownership Tips

- **Get Billing Right First**: A correct billing contact prevents payment issues and access interruptions.
- **Plan for Growth**: Keep 10-20% of seats unassigned for new hires.
- **Enable Auto-Renewal**: Critical employees should have auto-renewal to prevent unexpected access loss.
- **Monitor Expiries**: Set reminders for renewal dates—access interruptions hurt productivity.
- **Timezone Matters**: Get timezone right; it affects timekeeping and payroll calculations.
