# Welcome to Kando! 🚀

Hello and welcome to the team! Kando is your new home for everything related to your work life—tracking time, checking your schedule, requesting leave, and viewing your payslips.

This quick guide will help you get set up and running in about **15 minutes**.

---

## ✅ Your Onboarding Checklist

Complete these 5 steps to get fully set up:

### 1. [ ] Complete Your Profile
**Goal**: Ensure your personal details are correct.
- Go to **Settings** (gear icon) → **My Profile**.
- Verify your Name, Phone Number, and Emergency Contact.
- Upload a profile photo so your team can recognize you!
- *Time: 2 mins*

### 2. [ ] Set Notification Preferences
**Goal**: Don't miss important updates about shifts or leave approvals.
- Go to **Settings** → **Notifications**.
- Choose how you want to be notified (Email, SMS, or Mobile Push).
- We recommend enabling **Shift Changes** and **Leave Approvals**.
- *Time: 1 min*

### 3. [ ] Clock In for the First Time
**Goal**: Learn how to start your shift.
- Go to your **Dashboard**.
- Look for the big **Clock In** button at the top.
- Click it! You'll see a timer start running.
- (Don't worry, you can click **Clock Out** immediately if you're just testing).
- *Time: 1 min*

### 4. [ ] View Your Schedule
**Goal**: See when you're working next.
- Click on **Schedule** in the left menu.
- Look at the calendar view.
- Confirm your upcoming shifts for this week.
- *Time: 2 mins*

### 5. [ ] Check Your Leave Balance
**Goal**: See how much time off you have available.
- Click on **Leave** in the left menu.
- Look for the **My Balance** card.
- You should see your Vacation and Sick leave balances.
- *Time: 1 min*

## 💡 Quick Tips

- **Mobile App**: Did you know Kando has a mobile app? accurate-time-tracking and schedule-checking on the go! Ask your manager for the download link.
- **Forgot to Clock In?**: If you forget, don't panic! You can add a "Manual Time Entry" and your manager just needs to approve it.
- **Need Help?**: Click the **?** icon in the top right corner for support or check the [FAQ](../docs/7-Reference/7.3-FAQ.md).
