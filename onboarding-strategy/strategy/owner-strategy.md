# Owner Onboarding Strategy

## 🎯 Goal
Get the Organization Owner to **Complete organization profile, configure settings, verify billing, and assign initial seats** within the first 15 minutes.

## 👤 User Mindset
- "I need to set up my company information quickly."
- "How do I manage my subscription and avoid service interruptions?"
- "Who is responsible for billing and payments?"
- "How many users can I add and at what cost?"
- "What happens if a subscription expires?"

## 🗺️ The "Happy Path"

1. **Welcome Modal**: Intro to owner responsibilities and quick wins.
2. **Organization Profile Setup**: Guided form to enter company details and upload logo.
3. **Settings Configuration**: Simple settings wizard for timezone, date format, name format.
4. **Billing Verification**: Email verification confirmation or retry prompt.
5. **Subscription Overview**: Dashboard showing seat usage, expiry dates, and auto-renewal status.
6. **Quick Seat Assignment**: Assign first seat(s) to HR Admin or Manager to unlock team access.

## ✅ Onboarding Milestones (Checklist)
This checklist appears on the Owner Dashboard:

1. **Organization Profile**: Complete with name, address, and billing contact.
2. **Settings Configured**: Timezone, date format, and organizational preferences set.
3. **Billing Verified**: Billing email verified and ready to receive invoices.
4. **Subscription Reviewed**: Owner aware of seat count, usage, and expiry dates.
5. **Seats Assigned**: At least one seat assigned to team member or HR Admin.
6. **Auto-Renewal Enabled**: Critical seats set to auto-renew to prevent access loss.

## 💡 Convenience Features
- **Pre-filled Organization**: If created during signup, organization name should auto-populate.
- **Logo Upload Feedback**: Show immediate preview of logo as it will appear in system.
- **Email Verification Flow**: Auto-send verification email; show success/retry states clearly.
- **Subscription Summary Widget**: Dashboard widget showing "X of Y seats used", "Z expiring soon".
- **One-Click Auto-Renewal**: Bulk action to enable auto-renewal for all active seats.
- **Estimated Cost**: Show billing impact before assigning seats (per-seat cost × quantity).

## 🚨 High Drop-Off Points
- **Unverified Billing Email**: If not verified, show prominent warning. Owner may miss critical communications.
- **Confusing Seat Model**: Explain "1 seat = 1 user license for 1 module" clearly with examples.
- **Timezone Confusion**: Show what timezone means in context (payroll cutoff, timesheet day boundaries).
- **Subscription Expiry Surprise**: Alert prominently if seats are expiring soon or already expired.

## 🎓 Learning Outcomes
By end of owner onboarding, user should:
- ✅ Know their organization is properly configured (logo, name, billing).
- ✅ Understand timezone and date format impact on the system.
- ✅ Know how many users they can add (seats) and at what cost.
- ✅ Understand subscription renewal and auto-renewal benefits.
- ✅ Have assigned at least one seat to invite first team member.
- ✅ Feel confident billing won't surprise them (verified contact + invoices set up).

## 🔗 Interaction with Other Roles
- **After Owner Setup** → **HR Admin Onboarding**: HR Admin can now set up user roles and access groups.
- **HR Admin Creates Users** → **Users see their Role-Specific Onboarding** (Employee/Manager/etc).
- **Managers/Employees Onboard** → **Owner sees subscription usage increase** → Monitors seat renewal dates.

## 📊 Success Metrics
- Owner completes profile in <2 mins
- Owner configures settings in <3 mins
- Owner verifies billing in <2 mins
- Owner assigns first seat in <5 mins
- Owner enables auto-renewal (within first week)
- No unassigned seats left unused for >30 days
- Billing email verified before first invoice date
