# Manager Onboarding Strategy

## 🎯 Goal
Get the manager to **Review their Team** and **Approve/Reject a sample request** within the first 20 minutes.

## 👤 User Mindset
- "Who is on my team?"
- "Do I have any pending tasks/approvals?"
- "Is the schedule covered for next week?"
- "How do I see who is late/absent?"

## 🗺️ The "Happy Path"
1. **Welcome Modal**: Intro to Manager duties.
2. **Team Overview**: Guide them to the "My Team" widget.
3. **Action Training**: Walk them through a (potentially dummy) request approval flow.
4. **Scheduling Intro**: Show the "Team Schedule" view & how to assign a shift.
5. **Reporting**: Briefly point out where to find "End of Month" reports.

## ✅ Onboarding Milestones (Checklist)
This checklist appears on the Manager Dashboard:
1. **Review Team Roster**: Verify all team members are listed.
2. **Notifications**: Set up alerts for late clock-ins or leave requests.
3. **Process a Request**: Approve or reject a leave/time request.
4. **View Team Schedule**: Open the team calendar view.
5. **Generate a Report**: Run a test "Daily Attendance" report.

## 💡 Convenience Features
- **Pre-loaded Team**: Ensure their team members are already visible. Seeing "0 Members" is a high drop-off point.
- **Sample Request**: If no real requests exist, generate a "System Test Request" so they can practice the Approval workflow without fear.
- **Bulk Actions**: Highlight "Approve All" capabilities early so they know they don't have to click one-by-one forever.
